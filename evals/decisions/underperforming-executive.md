---
topic: fire or coach an underperforming senior executive hired a year ago
---
A growth-stage CEO hired a senior VP from a large company a year ago. The VP is well liked and politically skilled, but their organization keeps missing targets, decisions are slow, and two strong engineers have quit citing the VP. The CEO is tempted to give one more quarter with coaching because a replacement search would take months and the board approved the hire. The decision is whether to let the VP go now or invest in a turnaround plan.
