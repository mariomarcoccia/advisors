---
topic: reorganize a company around AI agents instead of hiring
---
A 40-person company believes AI agents can now do much of its support, QA, and data analysis work. The CEO is considering a hiring freeze and reorganizing teams so that each person manages agents rather than doing the work by hand, accepting short-term disruption and morale risk. The alternative is a slower, opt-in rollout of AI tools inside the current structure. They need to choose the pace and depth of the change.
