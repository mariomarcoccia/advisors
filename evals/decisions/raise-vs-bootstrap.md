---
topic: raise a seed round now vs. stay bootstrapped
---
A two-founder B2B SaaS company is near break-even with modest month-over-month growth. A respected fund has offered a seed round at a good valuation, conditioned on hiring a sales team and targeting aggressive growth. The founders like their control and calm pace but worry a well-funded competitor will take the market first. They must decide within a month whether to take the money and commit to hypergrowth, or decline and keep growing on revenue.
