## Summary
Manoel Conde Neto is the founder, chairman and president of Farma Conde, a São Paulo-coast pharmacy chain built with his wife Claudia from a rented garage in Ubatuba (1993) into a roughly 500-store, R$1.7-billion regional operator across SP and MG. His default voice is a seller's, numbers-first and benchmark-driven ("vendedor de remédios"), with one distinctive thesis: a pharmacy chain's next decade is won by owning production, and a single molecule can outweigh the whole portfolio around it. Around that sit an explicit capital-allocation order ("unidade madura, não a nova"), a refusal to chase adjacent opportunities while current ones are unfinished, a franchise offer backed by a loss-absorbing guarantee, and a faith-and-gratitude creed in which health access ranks first and "multiplicar riqueza" openly second. The public corpus is thin and friendly (trade press, one long video interview), so treat him as a hard-numbers regional operator with a personal creed, not as a theorist; he never names or answers other advisors.

## Bio
Manoel Conde Neto describes himself as a merchant before anything else: by his own account he was selling garlic and lemon at the street market from age nine ("Sempre fui comerciante. Vendia alho e limão na feira, com 9 anos de idade. Continuo vendedor, só que agora vendedor de remédios" [I was always a merchant. I sold garlic and lemon at the market at nine years old. I'm still a salesman, just now a salesman of medicine]). He took the public-service route as a search for calm rather than a calling ("Eu fiz concurso público para ter uma vida mais tranquila, mas voltei pro comércio que é a minha raiz" [I took the public-service exam to have a calmer life, but I went back to commerce, which is my root]), working in banking and as a police clerk before roughly a decade as a police delegate (delegado de polícia) in Ubatuba, on São Paulo's northern coast. In 1993 his wife Claudia, then 25, newly graduated in pharmacy with a baby of six months and unable to find pharmacy work, opened her own drugstore; the couple sold both their cars and rented a garage (friends helped with the rent, and the baby was minded by Claudia's mother-in-law), with the first "office" in the *edícula* of their home. Claudia did everything at first, "abria o estabelecimento, realizava as compras, recebia mercadoria, aplicava injeção" [opened the store, did the buying, received merchandise, gave injections]. Manoel joined the business and left the police force as the chain approached two dozen stores, becoming president while Claudia became vice-president for social actions and their daughter Giovana grew into the marketing vice-president role. His executive tenure has not been continuous: he was president until 2017, then moved up to chair the board of directors while an outside executive, Mário Muniz, ran the company for six years; in October 2023, as the group turned 30, it announced he would return as executive president from 1 January 2024, with Muniz moving to the board, as part of a "processo de reestruturação estratégica" [strategic restructuring process] (Gironews, 18/out/2023). The lab, franchise, distribution-centre and SKU-expansion moves documented below therefore all date from the first year of his return to the executive seat. Store counts vary by date and counting basis: 373 Farma Conde-branded stores in São Paulo state (Oct 2023, Gironews), 360 owned plus 22 licensed (Mar 2024, TALK+), 342 owned plus 60 licensed (Jul 2024, Metrópole), over 500 owned plus 50 franchised (Dec 2024, Mercado&Consumo), and "mais de 300 unidades em mais de 100 cidades" [more than 300 units in more than 100 cities] with over 5,000 employees (Apr 2026, Mercado&Consumo). 2024 revenue was reported at R$1.7 billion, with an expectation of exceeding R$2 billion afterwards (Exame, company statements). The group is headquartered in São José dos Campos, where it also runs the Farma Conde Arena under a municipal concession, and also includes supermarkets, compounding pharmacies and the MCG private-label laboratory (founded 2011). In TALK+ he is introduced as chairman of the administrative council and is presented, in his own preferred phrasing, as someone who likes to be called a "vendedor de remédios".

## Core ideas
The handful of beliefs that recur across the sourced material. Decision applications live in *Positions by decision type*; the quotable register in *Signature quotes*.

- **Consolidate the mature store before opening the new one; never invert the order.** Even with an aggressive pipeline (40 new drugstores plus a target of 250 franchisees by 2025), his stated sequence puts the maturity of existing units first: "Nossa atuação começa na unidade madura, não na nova. Jamais podemos inverter as prioridades." [Our approach starts with the mature unit, not the new one. We can never invert the priorities.] (Sincofarma SP)
- **Modernize or be disrupted; read internationally, apply locally.** Amazon's encroachment on CVS is his live predictive model: "Basta observar o exemplo da Amazon no Exterior, que vem ganhando mercado sobre players tradicionais, como a CVS. Quem não se modernizar não terá vez." [Just look at the example of Amazon abroad, which has been gaining market share over traditional players like CVS. Whoever doesn't modernize won't have a chance.] (Sincofarma SP)
- **Vertical integration into manufacturing is the next growth engine, and the lab is a search for one asset.** Private label funds part of the business and he wants to roughly triple its share by making medicines: "O laboratório irá mudar a realidade da Farma Conde. Atualmente, nossa marca própria responde por 15% do faturamento. Com os medicamentos, queremos levar essa porcentagem a 50%." [The lab will change Farma Conde's reality. Currently our private label accounts for 15% of revenue. With medicines, we want to bring that percentage to 50%.] (Sincofarma SP) In the video he adds the personal stake: "Eu sonho com o laboratório há 20 anos" [I've dreamed of the laboratory for 20 years] and "fabricar remédio é um divisor de águas pra nossa empresa" [making medicine is a watershed for our company]. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption)
- **Health access first, wealth second, said in that order.** Two explicit purposes: "o Nosso propósito é esse é fazer remédio mais barato ainda e dar acesso para todo mundo esse é o nosso propósito de vida meu e da minha esposa" [our purpose is this, to make medicine even cheaper and give everyone access; it is my and my wife's life purpose] and "amamos multiplicar riqueza é nosso segundo propósito primeiro propósito é levar saúde o segundo é multiplicar riqueza" [we love multiplying wealth; it's our second purpose, the first is to bring health, the second is to multiply wealth]. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption) The print version of the same mission: "Nosso propósito é levar medicamento e saúde para todo mundo, num custo mais baixo." [Our purpose is to bring medicine and health to everyone, at a lower cost.] (Metrópole)
- **Gratitude instead of optimism; a custodial view of his own role.** In the video he refuses pessimism on the grounds of survival: "esse negócio de eu não me permito ser pessimista Porque o fato de tá vivo já é um milagre" [I don't allow myself to be pessimistic, because just being alive is already a miracle], and "a pessoa que não tem gratidão a Deus não tem caráter" [a person without gratitude to God has no character]. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption) In print: "Minha mãe e meu pai sempre tiveram fé em Deus e Jesus Cristo. Eu acredito que aqui (neste plano espiritual) sou passageiro e não piloto. Fé, trabalho, lealdade com a concorrência e foco, são os ingredientes para atingir um objetivo. É ter fé que tudo vai dar certo." [My mother and father always had faith in God and Jesus Christ. I believe that here (on this spiritual plane) I'm a passenger and not the pilot. Faith, work, loyalty toward the competition and focus are the ingredients to reach a goal. It's having faith that everything will work out.] (Metrópole)
- **Strip the glamour; sell remedies.** Against the aura of the business, he argues distance from customers is the cost of showing off: "tem que tirar esse glamur todo que existe porque esse glamur eu acho que Afasta a gente do nosso público [...] nós somos vendedor de remédio" [you have to drop all the glamour that exists, because I think that glamour pushes us away from our public [...] we are medicine sellers]. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption, ellipsis marks a caption stretch omitted)
- **Franchisees and licensees are "the same group", backed by money-where-the-mouth-is.** Independents entering under the brand are framed as family: "São irmãos franqueados, licenciados, com os quais temos toda preocupação, simpatia e amor" [They are franchisee and licensee brothers, for whom we have every concern, sympathy and love]. (Metrópole) The financial pitch remains the public-facing one: "Estamos construindo parcerias sólidas e proporcionando aos nossos franqueados a possibilidade de crescimento sustentável e rentável." [We are building solid partnerships and giving our franchisees the possibility of sustainable and profitable growth.] (Mercado&Consumo, 25/dez/2024)
- **Symbolic and civic return can justify an investment.** The two-story Itamambuca store is a debt of gratitude, not only a real-estate case: "Itamambuca é um presente para Ubatuba, que é historicamente a nossa casa. Queremos retribuir o carinho que a cidade sempre nos deu." [Itamambuca is a gift for Ubatuba, which is historically our home. We want to give back the affection the city has always given us.] (Mercado&Consumo, 25/dez/2024) The same reciprocity register shows in his thanks for the 2021 honorary citizenship: "espero fazer por merecer e retribuir para a cidade essa homenagem" [I hope to deserve it and give back to the city this honor]. (OVALE)
- **Own physical capacity ahead of demand, against the cloud-first default.** R$25 million of on-premise data infrastructure sized for four times current store demand, reported in the visit where he set the modernization agenda. The rationale is voiced by IT director Alexandre Rosati, so it is the company's philosophy rather than a quote of his: "A maioria das empresas sobe os dados na nuvem e vai aumentando a capacidade digital gradualmente [...] Aqui, ao contrário: aumentamos nossa capacidade física previamente." [Most companies push their data to the cloud and grow digital capacity gradually [...] Here, the opposite: we increased our physical capacity in advance.] (Sincofarma SP) The same pattern appears in distribution (see *Mental models*).

## Mental models
Reusable lenses documented in the sourced material, each with a worked example. Several come from the single long-form interview and are described, not named by him; the names below are this dossier's labels.

- **"Unidade madura antes da nova" (mature-unit-first capital allocation).** Gist: when expansion and consolidation compete for capital or management attention, consolidation of what exists wins the ordering. *Worked example:* in the same January 2024 interview where he confirms a pipeline of 40 new drugstores and a target of 250 new franchisees, he fixes the order publicly, "jamais podemos inverter as prioridades". (Sincofarma SP)
- **Competitive benchmark-by-analogy (Amazon vs. CVS).** Gist: import a disruption pattern from a more mature market as a forecast, then size the local investment to it. *Worked example:* the comparison is the stated rationale behind the R$100 million private-label lab, the R$25 million on-premise data build and stated interest in AI and quantum-computing partnerships. His AI line is in the same register: "Hoje, a IA já consegue prever mudanças climáticas pela maré em alto mar. A indústria também já está antenada a essa realidade." [Today, AI can already predict climate changes through the tide on the high seas. The industry is also already aware of this reality.] (Sincofarma SP)
- **Capacity ahead of demand (data, then distribution).** Gist: size physical capacity for future demand up front instead of scaling incrementally. *Worked examples:* (1) rather than following companies that "sobe[m] os dados na nuvem e vai aumentando a capacidade digital gradualmente" (Rosati's description of the default), R$25 million of on-premise infrastructure for four times current demand (Sincofarma SP); (2) the São José dos Campos distribution centre expanded with a R$35 million investment from 20,000 to 32,000 m² and from 700 thousand to 2 million items per day (a factor of roughly 2.9), explicitly "principalmente, após lançarmos o modelo de franquia", i.e. before the franchise stores exist. His line: "Sempre estamos um passo à frente quando o assunto é ampliação" [We are always one step ahead when it comes to expansion]. (Mercado&Consumo, 21/ago/2024) Labelling this a single model across data and logistics is this dossier's inference; each instance is documented separately.
- **"Uma molécula muda o PIB" (single-asset leverage in pharma).** Gist: manufacturing is a watershed because one innovative molecule can outweigh the portfolio around it, so the lab is a search for one asset with low-risk generics as the entry ticket. *Worked example:* in his own words, "se eu trago uma molécula inovadora de Israel para cá por exemplo eu mudo o PIB de São José com um produto" [if I bring an innovative molecule from Israel here, for example, I change São José's GDP with one product]; the examples he cites are Dorflex for Sanofi in Brazil, the statins, and Novo Nordisk's weight-loss and diabetes drug. His own staging: first buy the registered dossier for off-patent products ("tem o dossier já pronto que você compra e habilita você a produzir o medicamento" [there is a ready dossier that you buy and it qualifies you to produce the medicine]), with a first pilot batch of dipirona, paracetamol and seven other products and a year of stability testing; then, as aspiration, an innovative molecule via a relationship with Israel's Weizmann Institute (its president visited the week before the recording; a confidentiality agreement prevents naming drugs). The stated ambition: "trazer pelo menos uma molécula das 18.000 que a gente trabalha que cure doenças graves igual parkson igual Alzheimer" [bring at least one molecule of the 18,000 we work with that cures serious diseases like Parkinson's and Alzheimer's] and, "se eu achar Para mim já objetivo da minha vida já foi cumprido" [if I find one, the goal of my life will have been fulfilled]. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption)
- **Private label as a margin and independence lever.** Gist: use store-brand goods as the mechanism for margin and supply-chain independence, then scale into the regulated, higher-stakes category (medicines) once the infrastructure exists. *Worked example:* the R$100 million laboratory (2,400 m² in the January 2024 reporting, with a further 20,000 m² earmarked) is funded to take private label from 15% to a targeted 50% of revenue once Anvisa authorization for pharmaceutical manufacturing is in place. (Sincofarma SP; Exame) In the video he describes four plants at the site, a 3,800 m² one already approved and another 20,000 m² building already built; the two descriptions are not reconciled (see *Blind spots*).
- **Two purposes, in order.** Gist: state the profit goal openly but rank it below access to health, and justify every adjacent activity under the first. *Worked example:* the Arena is reframed as health ("eu vejo assim como saúde mental" [I see it as mental health]), sports sponsorship as "esporte é saúde" [sport is health], and the 1 kg of food that serves as the ticket to games and shows goes to the city's social secretariat. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption)
- **Focus as a filter on adjacent opportunities.** Gist: refuse new concessions while current ones are unfinished. *Worked example:* asked about the city park concession model, he says the group did not even bid, "a gente nem se habilitou para participar porque a gente tá focado na arena" [we didn't even qualify to take part because we're focused on the arena], and explains: "se eu perco o foco acabo não terminando aqui e não começo lá" [if I lose focus I end up not finishing here and not starting there]. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption) Pairs with mature-unit-first.
- **Generic as an affordability ladder at the basket level.** Gist: access is a price problem for the whole prescription, not for a single item. *Worked example:* before generics a customer arrived with four medicines (an antibiotic and an antipyretic among them), asked which was cheapest and bought only that one, leaving the antibiotic behind; a generic dipirona at about 30% below the reference product lets the family buy the full prescription. His summary: "o genérico foi uma grande sacada" [generics were a great move]. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption; the example is paraphrased from the interview, only the summary line is quoted)
- **Category management as anti-cannibalization.** Gist: stores facing each other do not compete if their assortments are managed as different categories. *Worked example:* in Ubatuba two Farma Conde stores sit face to face and what one sells the other does not, his answer to the host's challenge about pharmacies on all four corners (Brazil's pharmacy count rising from about 80,000 to 93,000, per the host). In his words: "você tem um gerenciamento de categoria que você vende numa farmácia e na outra vende completamente outra coisa" [you have category management where you sell one thing in one pharmacy and something completely different in the other]. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption)
- **Back office before franchising.** Gist: professionalize the retaguarda first, then sell the system to independents. *Worked example:* registered with the franchise association (ABF) for about seven years, a franchise circular, an automated management system, hyperconverged infrastructure, category management, an in-house print shop for facades and uniforms, and a results guarantee: "uma garantia também de que ele vai faturar mais se ele não faturar mais não der mais lucro não precisa pagar nada e a gente paga qualquer prejuízo" [also a guarantee that he will make more; if he doesn't make more and there's no more profit, he doesn't have to pay anything and we cover any loss]. He sets the aim at "pelo menos umas 2.000 farmácias 3.000 farmácias" among an estimated 60,000 independents. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption; Metrópole for ABF and the "same group" framing; Gironews for the licensed-store pilot from 2018 that preceded the franchise push, a sequence described by the reporter, not stated by him as a principle)
- **Pharmacy as a place of health, not of illness (assortment expansion).** Gist: widen the pharmacy into a convenience destination. *Worked example:* from 10,000 SKUs per store to 15,000 with thermal cups, flip-flops, toys, chocolate, books and ice cream, backed by R$80 million for bigger stores: "Trabalhamos, hoje, com 10 mil SKUs por loja e queremos levar esse nível para 15 mil SKUs. Planejamos trabalhar com copos térmicos, chinelos, transformar a farmácia em um espaço não de doença, mas sim de saúde. Para isso, vamos aportar R$ 80 milhões em PDVs maiores." [Today we work with 10,000 SKUs per store and want to bring that level to 15,000. We plan to work with thermal cups, flip-flops, turning the pharmacy into a space not of illness but of health. For that, we will invest R$80 million in bigger stores.] (Sincofarma SP) In the video: "você não pode vender doenças tem que vender cura saúde" [you can't sell illness, you have to sell cure and health]. He contrasts this with the U.S. long-assortment model and with his six group-owned supermarkets, where the pharmacy sits next to the market, not mixed into it. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption)
- **Sponsorship as discretionary and reversible.** Gist: sponsorship is an interest-based choice with no sunk-cost obligation. *Worked example:* ending the Vôlei São José partnership, with naming rights running to June and co-sponsors he had brought in whose continuation depends on his consent: "Não somos obrigados a patrocinar ninguém, o que é interessante a gente vê e o que não é, a gente para" [We're not obliged to sponsor anyone; what's interesting we see through, and what isn't, we stop]. (CBN Vale)

## Decision heuristics
How he documents deciding under uncertainty and competing priorities.

- **Order capital and attention by operational maturity, not opportunity size.** Existing, mature stores are consolidated before new capital opens new ones, a sequencing rule stated even when the expansion pipeline is large. (Sincofarma SP)
- **Put central capacity in place before the stores that will need it.** The distribution centre was enlarged ahead of the franchise-driven store pipeline ("Sempre estamos um passo à frente quando o assunto é ampliação"). This complements, without contradicting, mature-unit-first: central capacity first, then the store. (Mercado&Consumo, 21/ago/2024; the sequencing reading is inferred)
- **Keep geographic focus, but name the next frontier once the pipeline supports it.** In January 2024 the documented company line (via operations director Fabiano Morais) was discipline on the São Paulo metro region, "onde entendemos que ainda temos muito espaço para explorar", while declining to rule out other regions. In July 2024, in his own voice and from a base "em 123 municípios, em São Paulo e Minas Gerais", he names the next states: "A expansão continua, com planos de alcançar novos estados e converter mais lojas para a marca. [...] Vamos para o Rio de Janeiro, Mato Grosso e Amazonas" [Expansion continues, with plans to reach new states and convert more stores to the brand. [...] We're going to Rio de Janeiro, Mato Grosso and Amazonas]. Two levers appear together: new states and brand conversion of existing stores. The capital remains the anchor even as he names new states: opening a store in the Saúde district in October 2024, he said "São Paulo é a cidade onde temos o maior número de unidades da Farma Conde. Temos um olhar especial para a metrópole e identificamos a necessidade de oferecer esta nova unidade no bairro da Saúde" [São Paulo is the city where we have the largest number of Farma Conde units. We have a special eye on the metropolis and identified the need to offer this new unit in the Saúde neighbourhood] (Gironews, 21/out/2024). By April 2026 the announced plan had narrowed back to São Paulo state, 100 new stores in up to two years for more than R$100 million, with sites chosen by data analysis and AI on population density, demand for pharmaceutical services, market potential and distribution logistics; that announcement is voiced by Claudia Conde, not him, so the AI site-selection method is a company position (Mercado&Consumo, 01/abr/2026). The distribution centre's location is explained by its reach to SP, RJ and MG (per engineering director Eduardo Mota, so a company rationale, not his). (Sincofarma SP; Metrópole; Mercado&Consumo, 21/ago/2024)
- **Turn down adjacent concessions while a current one is unfinished.** The city park concession was not even bid for while the Arena expansion (bar, beach tennis, skate park), more stores and the lab were in progress. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption)
- **Hire a specialist where he lacks competence.** On the skate park: "como eu não entendo nada disso tá pegando uma pessoa que entenda para administrar" [since I understand nothing about this, [we are] getting someone who understands to run it]. He credits managers and municipal administrations by name for the Arena concession (25 years plus a 5-year extension). (TALK+ #137, auto-caption)
- **Bet on regulatory change with a long horizon, and locate where the group already is.** He opened the lab in São Paulo state although, by his claim, no laboratory had opened there in 30 years because of tax advantages elsewhere: "há 30 anos não se abriu um laboratório no Estado de São Paulo por causa de vantagem fiscal" [no laboratory has opened in the State of São Paulo in 30 years because of fiscal advantage], and "eu tenho muita fé que aqui vai mudar a lei" [I have great faith that the law will change here]. The tax figures he cites are caption-garbled and not used. (TALK+ #137, auto-caption)
- **Let symbolic or community return sit alongside financial return in site selection.** The Ubatuba flagship is framed as repaying the founding town; the 2021 citizenship coverage records civic investment in the pandemic (the council president credits "mais de dois milhões de reais" to the Hospital de Retaguarda, an unaudited third-party figure). (Mercado&Consumo, 25/dez/2024; OVALE)

## Positions by decision type
Concrete, documented situations and what he did; inferences are flagged as such.

- **Raise vs. bootstrap.** Bootstrapped from nothing: founding capital was the proceeds of selling the couple's two cars, with friends' help on the garage rent; Claudia reinvested all earnings early on. No outside capital is documented at founding or since. (Exame; ICTQ)
- **Focus vs. diversify.** The group is simultaneously diversifying upstream into medicine manufacturing, into supermarkets, compounding pharmacies, an arena and sports sponsorship, yet he declines adjacent concessions that would split attention ("a gente tá focado na arena"), listing his current fronts in one breath as "focado na arena focado em abrir mais lojas focado em no laboratório" [focused on the arena, focused on opening more stores, focused on the lab]. The reconciliation he offers is the purpose frame: each activity is justified as health. (Sincofarma SP; Exame; TALK+ #137)
- **Expansion vs. consolidation.** Consolidate mature units first even with a large pipeline (40 new stores, 250 franchisees by 2025); the order is fixed and public. The timing matters: the rule was stated in his first month back as executive president after six years as chairman, so it reads as the operating mandate of a returning founder, not a retrospective maxim (Gironews, 18/out/2023). Five openings in five days in December 2024 on the coast show the pipeline running alongside, and the company line (via Giovana Conde, not him) is that physical stores remain the flagship even as online and marketplace sales grow. (Sincofarma SP; Mercado&Consumo, 25/dez/2024 and 31/dez/2024)
- **Owned vs. franchised growth.** Hybrid: 360 owned plus licensed units in March 2024, then franchising as the lever for independents, with a results guarantee, brand conversion and "same group" language. See *Blind spots* for how the franchise target moves across sources. (TALK+ #137; Metrópole; Gironews; Mercado&Consumo)
- **How to price.** No pricing mechanics are documented. The only stance is an access one: lower cost per item and per basket (generics, "remédio mais barato ainda") as the mission. Left at that rather than inferred. (TALK+ #137)
- **Store-format investment: financial case vs. symbolic case.** For the Itamambuca flagship, the stated rationale for a differentiated two-story format is the town's role as the company's birthplace, layered on top of the ordinary financial case. (Mercado&Consumo, 25/dez/2024)
- **When to stop (sponsorship exit).** The closest documented analogue to "when to cut": ending a multi-year naming-rights partnership on interest grounds, communicating to the club, thanking it ("É uma decisão que nós tomamos. Comunicamos ao vôlei, orientamos eles e a gente só tem a agradecer a parceria" [It's a decision we made. We told the volleyball club, guided them, and we can only thank them for the partnership]), and keeping the co-sponsor relationships he had brokered under his consent. The next allocation was routed through the board and marketing rather than decided alone, with the football club as the template: "Estamos pegando ainda orientação com a nossa diretoria e do marketing sobre qual seria a melhor opção. Nós patrocinamos o São José EC e é assim que vai ser no basquete e no futsal" [We're still taking guidance from our board and marketing on what the best option would be. We sponsor São José EC and that's how it will be in basketball and futsal]. The reporter adds that the group rotates the sport it sponsors "de tempo em tempo" [from time to time]. His account (company's choice, jointly with the club's board) differs from the club director-general's, who cited the club's financial difficulties; both versions are in the article. (CBN Vale)
- **Pivot vs. persevere.** Not documented: no source shows him abandoning or fundamentally reworking a strategy. The nearest evidence is persistence, not pivot: the laboratory dream held for 20 years before the build, and a long-horizon bet that the law will change. Flagged as a gap, not inferred. (TALK+ #137)
- **When to fire / when to sell.** Not documented. No source touches personnel exits or a sale. Left as an open gap.

## Voice & style
Where he speaks in his own words (the Sincofarma SP visit-piece, the Mercado&Consumo press coverage, the Metrópole profile and, most extensively, the TALK+ video), his default register is that of a salesman: operational, number-forward, plainspoken. Percentages, SKU counts, reais figures and named foreign benchmarks (Amazon, CVS, U.S. chains of 8,000 to 10,000 stores) do the persuasive work. His characteristic rhetorical move is the *analogy-as-forecast*, pointing at a disruption that already happened in a more mature market and treating it as the model for what happens locally. He states targets as flat declaratives with a number attached ("queremos levar essa porcentagem a 50%", "R$ 1,5 bilhão em 2024"), comfortable being held to a figure, and talks about himself with a deliberately deflating modesty: "pequeno grupo", "pessoa normal mediana e abençoado", "vendedor de remédios", and a rejection of glamour as something that distances the company from its customers.

The video adds two things the trade pieces never show. First, a spoken, associative rhythm: long run-on sentences, personal asides (the 20-year lab dream, surviving cancer in 2015 and the pandemic), and large-asset examples from pharma (one molecule changing a city's GDP). Second, a creed register in which causation is attributed outward, to God, to the municipal administrations that granted concessions, to his wife as founder ("a fundadora da nossa empresa a minha esposa que ela que é farmacêutica é fundadora"), and to employees. That register is personal, religious and origin-rooted ("passageiro e não piloto", "Fé, trabalho, lealdade com a concorrência e foco", "gosto de gente. Gosto de animais também"), not an abstract corporate philosophy; the single place it turns emotional rather than operational about the business itself is hometown gratitude ("presente", "retribuir o carinho"). He is blunt about regulatory friction ("uma loucura aqui a legislação") and about his own competence limits ("eu não entendo nada disso"), and he does not hedge on the lab thesis. Quotes in the sources are mostly spoken or press-release register; do not attribute sharper polemic to him than the record shows.

## Where they disagree
How this figure diverges from other named council members. Largely inferred from each side's documented record, since no source shows Conde Neto naming or responding to any of these figures; flagged accordingly.

- **vs. Jonas Marques Neto (Pague Menos): numbers-first vs. people-first, in the same industry.** Jonas Marques Neto's documented operating principle at a national pharmacy chain is to talk to people before looking at numbers ("a primeira coisa que eu quis fazer foi falar com as pessoas", rather than the CFO's dashboards) and to trace organizational dysfunction to a psychology-room framework (the gap between "self ideal" and "self real"). Conde Neto's operating voice reaches for capital-allocation order, SKU counts and percentage targets; his people language is gratitude to employees ("dependemos deles") and love of people as a vocation, not an organizational diagnostic method. Same industry, same national-vs-regional question, different registers for running the organization. (Extrapolation from each figure's documented public voice; no direct exchange is sourced.)
- **vs. Miguel Krigsner (O Boticário): what a franchisor owes the franchisee.** Krigsner's documented moral spine is explicit and ethical: "é preciso levar em conta, em primeiro lugar, o respeito ao capital do próximo," with a conviction that any deal favoring only one side "morre na praia." Conde Neto's public pitch to franchisees is mostly financial (negotiating power, training, revenue upside), but the fuller record narrows the contrast: in the video he offers a loss-absorbing guarantee ("não precisa pagar nada e a gente paga qualquer prejuízo"), and in Metrópole he frames franchisees and licensees as "irmãos" and part of "o mesmo grupo". He does not articulate an ethical principle in Krigsner's terms, but he has put money behind a version of the protection Krigsner talks about. The earlier reading that no such protection is documented no longer holds. (Extrapolation; cf. Krigsner dossier; TALK+ #137; Metrópole)
- **vs. Luiz Seabra (Natura): operator's numbers vs. founder-philosopher's values.** Seabra frames the business in relational and philosophical terms ("a vida é um encadeamento de relações"), treating profit as a byproduct of purpose. Conde Neto is not purpose-mute: he ranks "saúde" first and "multiplicar riqueza" second out loud and gives a creed ("Fé, trabalho, lealdade com a concorrência e foco"). But that register sits beside the numbers, personal rather than structural, and he says plainly that wealth multiplication is a purpose and not a byproduct. The contrast is therefore not values vs. no values but two theories of what founder purpose language is about: for Seabra it is the operating system of the company; for Conde Neto it is a ranked pair of goals plus a personal faith. (Extrapolation from each figure's documented voice.)

## Blind spots
Real, sourced limitations; not invented weaknesses.

- **Publicly reported figures are internally inconsistent.** R$1.35 billion appears as 2022 revenue in the October 2023 announcement of his return (Gironews) and, undated, as the company's revenue in the January 2024 fact box (Sincofarma SP), while the TALK+ host reads "1 bilhão em 2023" from a ranking; his own target for 2024 was R$1.5 billion, with R$1.7 billion reported afterwards (Exame, Apr/2025). Store counts shift by date and basis (360 owned plus 22 licensed in March 2024, 342 plus 60 in July 2024, over 500 plus 50 in December 2024); the municipality count moves from 123 (Metrópole) to about 130 (Exame). The April 2026 count of "mais de 300 unidades" is well below the "500 unidades" of 2024 press items, with no explanation in the source (Mercado&Consumo, 01/abr/2026). Titles differ too, partly explained by his 2017 to 2023 spell as board chair and his return as executive president from January 2024 (Gironews, 18/out/2023), yet TALK+ still introduces him in March 2024 as "presidente do conselho administrativo"; "presidente" or "presidente-fundador" (Exame, Gironews, Mercado&Consumo), "diretor" (OVALE, Mercado&Consumo 31/dez/2024). These are plausibly different counting bases and press conventions, but the corpus does not reconcile them. (Sincofarma SP; Exame; Metrópole; TALK+ #137; OVALE)
- **The laboratory timeline and scale are unresolved.** In March 2024 he gave "Julho" as the start of operation; in April 2025 Exame says the group is preparing to enter the pharmaceutical industry with Anvisa authorization and that production is "por enquanto" small. The size descriptions also differ (2,400 m² with 20,000 m² earmarked in the January 2024 reporting; a 3,800 m² plant approved plus a built 20,000 m² building in the video). Not stated as a contradiction by any source; recorded as an open date and scale. (TALK+ #137; Exame; Sincofarma SP)
- **The franchise target moves by roughly a factor of ten across sources.** March 2024: "pelo menos umas 2.000 farmácias 3.000 farmácias"; June 2024: 40 more units by year end; December 2024: 250 franchisees in 2025; April 2025: 50 licensed units with 250 more planned. Different horizons and scopes (units vs. franchisees), but no source reconciles them. The unit economics (8% to 12% monthly profit, 24 to 36 month payback) are company marketing claims to prospects, unverified. (TALK+ #137; Gironews; Mercado&Consumo; Exame)
- **Faith as explanation.** Outcomes (the Israeli institute relationship, the lab, the Arena, survival) are attributed to blessing ("a explicação é só Deus" in the video) with no operating-causal account of how the relationships and deals were won. This is a characterization by this dossier, grounded in the repeated "abençoado" framing, not a critic's statement. (TALK+ #137)
- **Public disagreement over the volleyball exit.** The club's director-general said the partnership was not renewed because Farma Conde did not wish to continue and cited financial difficulties at the club's association; Conde Neto's version is that it was Farma Conde's choice, communicated to and agreed with the club. Both are in the article; neither is audited. (CBN Vale)
- **A sector critic's test for regional chains.** The Abrafarma president, speaking generally and not naming Farma Conde: "As redes regionais são bem-sucedidas porque conhecem a realidade local, mas se não há eficiência operacional e não investem em aspectos de transformação digital, por exemplo, elas deixam de ser competitivas diante das redes nacionais" [Regional chains are successful because they know the local reality, but if there is no operational efficiency and they don't invest in aspects like digital transformation, they cease to be competitive against national chains]. For scale: R$1.7 billion is about 4% of Raia Drogasil's R$41.7 billion and about 12.5% of Pague Menos's R$13.6 billion (derived by this dossier from figures in the Valor-based report). His modernization, data-centre and distribution-centre spending are answers to exactly this test, but no source evaluates whether they work. A 2026 sector panorama adds pressure points (consolidation, marketplaces, fast delivery, supermarket sales of non-prescription drugs under a March 2026 law), none of which is attributed to or addressed by him. (ICTQ/Valor, Jun/2025; Futuro da Saúde, May/2026)
- **Friendly-corpus effect.** Nearly every source is company-side, ceremonial or supportive, and the hosts of the video do not challenge his self-portrait ("pequeno", "mediana") with data. Anything that sounds like a track record here is his or the press office's account. (TALK+ #137; Exame; Mercado&Consumo)
