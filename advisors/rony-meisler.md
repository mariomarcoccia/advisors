## Summary

Rony Meisler is the co-founder of Reserva, the irreverent Brazilian menswear brand he bootstrapped from R$6,000 and door-to-door shorts into a roughly R$2B AR&Co before a turbulent 2024 exit from Azzas 2154 (the 2020 sale to Arezzo&Co was ~R$715M, mostly in locked-up shares); he now runs Rebels Ventures, a club-deal investor and self-described "copiloto" in consumer brands, media and entrepreneur education, sells the "Manual de Donos" newsletter school, and chairs Conscious Capitalism Brazil. His lens is **brand-as-movement and brand as the scarce asset when everything commoditizes**: audience, then community, then product (the ACP funnel) with near-zero launch risk; the customer, and the salesperson closest to them, as the real boss; cash over the P&L; and purpose as a profit engine, argued against Milton Friedman by name. He is contrarian on disruption worship, the lone genius, the do-what-you-love creed (he says do what you are good at), mentoring and info-product gurus, and Silicon Valley dividend orthodoxy (he now says founders should take dividends and live as owners), and he is openly a curator-synthesist who credits Sinek, Youngme Moon, Jack Welch, Rory Sutherland and his father. He aligns with brand-builders like caito-maia, luiz-seabra and, personally, his self-described "madrinha" luiza-helena-trajano, and clashes with numbers-first operators (frederico-trajano), centralizing controllers (the Birman split, though he now says "nunca teve briga"), and Friedman-style investors; the real tensions are a founder fused to his brand and a control friction he admits to, a history of brand controversies defended on intent first (2014 to 2022) plus the 2023 Fluminense "pó de arroz" suit, theatrical pranks beside an authenticity thesis, and many self-reported numbers that drift from one telling to the next.

## Bio

Rony Meisler (b. March 4, 1981, Rio de Janeiro) is a production engineer by training (PUC-RJ) who became one of Brazil's best-known brand entrepreneurs. He and advertising-trained childhood friend Fernando Sigal noticed, at a gym, five men wearing the identical pair of shorts without registering it, the moment he calls **"O dia D"** (for "demanda reprimida" [pent-up demand]), and read it as evidence of a "multidão faminta" [hungry crowd] of Brazilian men underserved by fashion (blog, "É para isso que eu pago a internet"). In 2006 (Catraca Livre, Poder360 and meusucesso say 2004; his own dated statements, his LinkedIn profile and the 18th anniversary he marked on 6 September 2024 point to 2006) they founded **Reserva** with just R$6,000 ("Começamos nosso negócio em 2006 apenas com R$ 6 mil e esse foi o total da grana que investimos até hoje", "Don't look up!"), selling shorts door to door, then to multi-brand resellers, before opening a first 33m² store on Rua Maria Quitéria in Ipanema, later enlarged to 66m² ("Don't look up!"). His father, Luiz Meisler (Oracle's VP for Latin America), had talked him out of over-engineered projections: "Esqueçam essa planilha e vão para rua criar produtos incríveis" (blog, "Roube um estilo de liderança"). Twelve years after the first store, Fast Company named Reserva among the world's most innovative companies alongside the "big 4," citing 1P=5P (hunger relief), a 45-day paternity leave, the customer-customization system *faça.vc*, print-on-demand, and *"cara ou coroa"*, a program employing people over 70 in its stores ("Don't look up!"). By 2019 revenue reached ~R$400M.

In December 2020 **Arezzo&Co acquired Reserva for ~R$715M**. His own account of the rationale is a market-sizing one: Reserva's brand awareness was "AB+" but its retail reach far narrower, making the addressable market "10 vezes maior" [10x bigger] under a bigger platform (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions). He became CEO of **AR&Co** (Reserva, Reserva Mini, Reserva Go, Reserva Ink, Eva, Oficina, later Baw), co-led a ~R$900M follow-on at R$83/share with Alexandre Birman, and grew the division from about R$400M (2019) to a reported ~R$2B (Sicomércio and Startups.com.br coverage; the company's own August 2024 communiqué cited R$1.6B through June 2024, per Bloomberg Línea), with margins he describes as rising from 10 to 12% to 16 to 17% (self-reported, Futurum Talks). At the sale his father warned him that founders who sell often "sabem que elas venderam, mas elas não entendem que elas venderam" [know they sold it but don't understand they sold it], advice he says he tried to live by for four years.

He left Azzas 2154 in 2024: the departure was announced on 29 August 2024, the founders stayed "até o final deste ano" to support the transition (Azzas's wording, per Bloomberg Línea), and he dates his own exit to 31 December 2024 (Entusiasta, unofficial captions; see *Provenance notes*). The press framed it as turbulent, with a compensation gap and a centralization clash with Birman after the Arezzo and Soma merger; he frames it as knowing when to stop and says there was never a fight (see *Positions by decision type* and *Blind spots*). A failing health checkup (pre-diabetic, pre-hypertensive, ~20kg overweight, high cortisol; he dates it to the end of 2023) became the trigger for **Rebels Ventures** (2025), a hybrid investment/advisory/venture-builder that began in wellness and longevity, run with Fernando Sigal, José Alberto Silva and Jayme Nigri Moszkowicz. He has chaired **Conscious Capitalism Brazil** since 2016 (alongside Hugo Bethlem), and co-authored **"Rebeldes Têm Asas"** with journalist Sérgio Pugliese (2017; special edition with a bonus chapter and a preface by Luiza Helena Trajano, Editora Gente, 2021, which debuted at no. 1 on the PublishNews general list with 5,540 copies in a week).

**After Azzas.** He took about 30 days off in California with his family before producing content (Entusiasta, unofficial captions), staged the "Doing Nothing Club" prank, and launched Rebels Ventures publicly in 2025 with a first announced bet on The Simple Gym (Exame, May 2025; Fitfeed, June 2025). By July 2026 he reports eight portfolio companies, R$78M invested from partners' own capital, a personal-brand newsletter ("E-mail do Rony"), a paid email school for small owners ("Manual de Donos"), and a fashion non-compete that ends on 4 December 2026 (Café com Ferri; Excepcionais, unofficial captions). Before entrepreneurship he had only a brief Accenture internship and no executive role; the Startups.com.br summary reports that he called becoming an executive after the sale "o maior desafio da vida até aqui". In September 2024 he marked Reserva's 18th anniversary with a pay-what-you-want sale of the book (R$1.80, R$18 or R$180; 1,800 copies; all proceeds to social projects, with promised accountability), his own promotional text (Substack, Skeelo).

## Core ideas

- **Purpose is a profit engine, not a cost center.** *"O maior propulsor de valor e alta performance para o negócio é o propósito"* [purpose is the greatest driver of value and high performance for a business] (Sicomércio 2025); reaffirmed after leaving Azzas: *"o propósito não só pode como deve ser a maior válvula motora da alta performance"* (Consumidor Moderno, CONAREC 2025).
- **A brand is a movement and a community before it is a product.** *"No mundo do empreendedorismo da atenção, as marcas mais valiosas não começarão criando produtos e sim comunidades."* (blog, "Community is the new king").
- **Innovation's raw material is listening, not technology, his single most repeated line.** *"A matéria-prima da inovação não é a tecnologia. É a audição."* (blog, "Don't look up!", 2023), restated nearly verbatim in "Tinta amarela" (2023), "Gigantes, pequenas revoluções" (2024), ExpoSuper 2025, and as the second of Rebels Ventures' three named values in 2026 ("mente aberta," TalksbyLeo #238). Five years of continuity on one idea.
- **You are a company of people who sell, not a company that sells to people.** *"Não somos uma empresa que pretende vender roupas para pessoas e sim uma empresa de pessoas que vendem roupas."* and *"Somos muito mais uma empresa de comunicação de marca do que uma empresa de moda."* [We are much more a brand-communication company than a fashion company] (his LinkedIn profile, Grupo Reserva entry, self-description).
- **Execution beats ideation; imperfection is a starting condition.** *"Costumo dizer que a execução é muito mais importante que a idealização"* (Suno); reaffirmed at CONAREC 2025: *"É preciso começar. O perfeito nasce tarde; melhor é começar e depois melhorar."*
- **Authenticity is strategic, not just moral**, and a live tension. *"A autenticidade não é só mais humana. É mais inteligente."* (blog, "Déjà vu"). See *Blind spots* for cases (an undisclosed-until-the-reveal AI-ghostwritten column, a "prank" launch) that sit uneasily against this.
- **Discipline is freedom; time is the only real asset.** He adopts Jocko Willink's *"Disciplina é liberdade."* as the frame for his anti-distraction routine (blog, "O presente é o passado do futuro"); *"A verdade é que somos todos bilionários. O ativo mais valioso do mundo não é o dinheiro. É o tempo."* [We are all billionaires. The world's most valuable asset isn't money, it's time] (blog, "Eu sou um bilionário").
- **Success is more dangerous than failure.** *"O fracasso te obriga a mudar. O sucesso te convida a ficar onde está."* [Failure forces you to change. Success invites you to stay where you are], his gloss on management scholar James G. March's exploitation-vs-exploration research: success *"se disfarça de inteligência"* [disguises itself as intelligence] (LinkedIn Pulse, "Cuidado com o sucesso," March 2026).
- **Business must pursue purpose *and* profit, not choose.** *"Não se trata de ter propósito ou lucro, mas de perseguir propósito e lucro."* (blog, "Friedman estava completamente errado"), his explicit rebuttal of shareholder-primacy doctrine (see *Where they disagree*).

- **Brand is the scarce asset when everything commoditizes.** *"No mundo em que tudo vai se comoditizar, o que vai ser rei? Marca. Se você tem marca, você consegue estabelecer uma marca forte e usa bem a tecnologia e o serviço para escalar essa marca nesse mundo que surge"* [In a world where everything gets commoditized, what will be king? Brand...] (Startups.com.br, podcast MVP summary). In the spoken versions: *"marca é o novo petróleo"* and *"quem tem marca é rei"* (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions). The Rebels thesis is built on it (see *Mental models*).
- **The customer is the boss, and sales is the survival function.** *"Quem manda num negócio é o cliente."* [The customer runs a business.] (Entusiasta, unofficial captions). *"Só a venda salva. Vai pra rua vender."* [Only selling saves you. Go out and sell.] and *"Quem sabe qual é o seu produto é o cliente."* [The customer is who knows what your product is.] (Powercast #248, unofficial captions). The same line closes his advice to a beginner in Café com Ferri: *"Vai pra rua vender, cara, qualquer merda."* (unofficial captions).
- **Retail breaks on cash, not on the P&L.** *"Varejo no Brasil ele não quebra no resultado, ele quebra no caixa."* [Retail in Brazil doesn't break on profit, it breaks on cash.] (Café com Ferri, unofficial captions). He dates the instinct to his grandfather, who in his telling "quebrou no caixa" (Futurum Talks #56, unofficial captions), and says it took him years to internalize that cash, not EBITDA, keeps a company alive (Startups podcast, unofficial captions).
- **Time ownership is the real wealth, and being an executive cost him it.** On the four years after the sale: *"alguém era dono do meu tempo e não eu. E isso me matou, cara."* [someone else owned my time, not me, and that killed me] and *"o problema não era trabalhar, o problema era não ser dono do meu tempo"* (Entusiasta, unofficial captions). On why he left: *"a decisão ela não foi de saída, ela não foi financeira, a decisão foi para recuperar isso"* [the decision wasn't about leaving or about money, it was about recovering this] (Excepcionais, unofficial captions). Compare the written version in *Eu sou um bilionário* above.
- **Start with what you are good at, not what you love.** *"faz o que você é bom"* [do what you are good at]: in the first phase of a company "faz o que você ama" is, in his view, a myth, and small daily wins release the dopamine that creates the love afterwards: *"Se você fizer o que você é bom, quando você vencer a primeira, as vitórias pequenas do dia a dia, vai liberar a dopamina"* (ROI Hunters #349, unofficial captions). This reverses an older line used to promote his talks: *"O empreendedor não perde tempo pensando em que negócio investir, ele faz uma paixão se tornar algo rentável"* [the entrepreneur doesn't waste time choosing a business; he makes a passion profitable] (PANROTAS event announcement, January 2025, an undated pull quote). Treat the 2026 spoken version as current.
- **Take dividends; get rich from the business, not from its sale.** *"o empreendedor tem que tirar dividendo"* [the entrepreneur has to take dividends] (ROI Hunters #349) and *"vocês têm que enriquecer com o negócio, vocês não tem que enriquecer com a venda do negócio"* [you have to get rich from the business, not from selling it] (Excepcionais). He adds, of his own case, that it was not so at Reserva (see *Positions by decision type*). Both unofficial captions.
- **Purpose is discovered by trying and failing, not decreed in a meeting.** *"A palavra do momento é o propósito."* and *"Agora, quando é a pessoa jurídica, ela acha que vai entrar em uma sala de reunião e já sair de lá com o propósito. Mas não é assim, é preciso errar para aprender"* [Companies think they'll walk into a meeting room and walk out with a purpose. It doesn't work like that; you have to err to learn.] His analogy: an individual finds what fulfils them through years of therapy (PANROTAS 2025, reporter-attributed quotes). This refines, and partly softens, the purpose-as-profit-engine line above.
- **Innovation is small, daily and survivable; disruption worship is a trap.** *"dificilmente alguém que está nessa cidade ou País vai inventar o próximo instagram."* [hardly anyone in this city or country will invent the next Instagram], *"Não precisa ter uma agência de viagens monstruosa para ser inovador"* and *"quando a gente erra é que a gente aprende. Não tem como inovar se não errar."* [we learn when we get it wrong; you can't innovate without getting it wrong] (PANROTAS 2025, to a travel-agency audience; the reporter paraphrases that many people feel obliged to disrupt the market and see themselves as losers if they don't).
- **Stakeholder capitalism is a longevity claim, not only a moral one.** *"As empresas que estarão vivas daqui a 200, 300 ou 400 anos são as que pregam o capitalismo de stakeholder e não de shareholder"* and *"O dia que eu estiver aqui para olhar apenas o resultado financeiro, eu não verei mais sentido no negócio"* [The day I am here only to look at the financial result, I will no longer see meaning in the business] (Diário do Comércio column by Juliane Maciel, a Conscious Capitalism ambassador, who says she took the lines from an IstoÉ Dinheiro interview that this pass could not locate; treat as second-hand, from the period when he was still AR&Co CEO).
- **Never depend on other people's money.** Rony traces the rule to a failed corporate-barter website he built at 18, when an investor who had promised funds backed out in the dot-com bubble: *"Nunca mais ia depender dinheiro de ninguém para fazer minhas coisas."* [I would never again depend on anyone's money to do my things.] (Entusiasta, unofficial captions). His father's version, as he retells it: *"Vai inventar um modelo que se sustenta, porque dinheiro de pai e nem de banco você vai tomar"* [Invent a model that sustains itself, because you won't get money from your father or from a bank] (Café com Ferri, unofficial captions).

## Mental models

- **Audience → Community → Product (ACP funnel).** Build the audience, convert it into a community with shared values, then launch products that funnel already wants, near-zero CAC. *Worked example:* he calls it *"um funil que é mais velho que a nossa avó"* [a funnel older than our grandmother] but says Reserva's entire management model *"Era ancorado no ACP, porque assim a gente garante que a empresa inteira trabalha pro cliente"* [was anchored in ACP, so the whole company works for the customer], and that the same audience-first logic sourced Rebels Ventures' first investment: *"Eu comecei a treinar o algoritmo para me mostrar negócios assim"* [I started training the algorithm to show me businesses like that], until The Simple Gym appeared in his timeline as a lead rather than a pitch (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions). Caveat, from the "Community is the new king" post: *"quando o “creator” é também empreendedor, a história muda"*, audience alone isn't a business.

- **Three leverages: Capital, Code, Content.** Scaling needs leverage: Capital (not accessible to all), Code (micro-SaaS, cheaper via AI), Content (most accessible). *Worked example, the "vibe coding" pivot:* a Rebels-backed educator had emotional attachment to a "Micro SaaS" course brand priced to sell at R$97 "one shot"; Meisler talked him into repositioning around AI "vibe coding" and repricing, by his account the relaunch sold nearly R$2 million in 48 hours (captions: *"A gente vendeu quase R$ 2 milhõesais em 48 horas, Léo."*, followed by *"O Bruno vendeu."*; TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions, founder-reported). This is Content/Code leverage exercised directly on a portfolio company, not just stated as thesis.

- **Rebels Ventures' operating model: "copiloto," not owner.** Investment + advisory + venture-builder, structured as a **club deal** (each partner sizes their own check per deal, no obligation) on bootstrapped businesses already at market fit (the bands moved over time, see *Positions by decision type*), deliberately *not* Rocket-Internet-style building from zero. *Worked example:* *"É o mesmo framework de quando estávamos na Reserva e depois na AR&Co. Mas quem manda no negócio é o empreendedor. Estaremos como copilotos se ele precisar"* (NeoFeed, Rebels exclusive), with the four partners split by function (Rony on brand, Jayme on operations, Fernando on supply chain, José on digital). His limits are under *Selection by edge* below; how the copilot role works in practice is under *Rebels' operating mode*.

- **CX as a variable cost / journey reverse-engineering.** Treat experience design as a margin lever: *"Este varejista que vos escreve sempre viu CX como custo variável."* Sequence the journey so high-margin, customizable choices come first (blog, "A ordem dos fatores...").

- **"Seja a cola" (be the glue): the Battier effect.** The most valuable operator is the connective, unglamorous force who makes the team better, not the solo star. *Worked example:* NBA role-player Shane Battier, *"Quando eu ajudava os outros a vencer, eu deixava de ser o estranho. Eu me tornava parte."* (blog, "Seja a cola"). The thesis sits in friction with his own admitted difficulty ceding decisions at Rebels (see *Blind spots*).

- **Innovation ≠ technology; validate cheap before you scale capital, "tinta amarela."** *Worked example:* a store-display manager wanted to repaint every storefront yellow at a cost of "algumas centenas de milhares de reais," certain it would lift sales. Instead of funding it directly, Meisler had her test-paint only the chain's *worst-performing* store, brokered a bulk discount from a paint brand contingent on the rollout, and let the pilot's result (the store *"dobrou o faturamento em muito pouco tempo"*) decide the full spend. *"Tem gente que gosta de chamar esse teste de MVP, mas eu gosto de chamar de teste a custo baixo mesmo."* (blog, "Tinta amarela").

- **Turn the catastrophe into the next business.** *Worked example, the "Use Huck" disaster (about 2014; meusucesso, writing in September 2021, says "Há sete anos"):* a co-branded custom-tee venture with Luciano Huck accidentally published an adult-shirt artwork over a *child's* photo; *"Ela ficou vinte minutos no ar, mas quando tiramos já tinha se formado um tsunami de críticas"* [it was up for twenty minutes, but by the time we took it down a tsunami of criticism had formed] in the meusucesso account (his 2025 spoken retelling says "30 segundos" [30 seconds]; see *Provenance notes*), detonated a backlash, and effectively killed the partnership. In Marcas Rebeldes #01 (unofficial captions) he adds that the partner asked "quanto eu te devo?" and he answered that he owed nothing, only that they avoid layoffs and redeploy people and equipment, which became Reserva Inc. Instead of laying off the 30 people on it (*"Em qualquer companhia, elas seriam mandadas embora."*), Meisler repurposed the operation into a white-label T-shirt service for any content brand that wanted a shop, which grew to roughly **10× the original** (meusucesso, "Os erros e acertos").

- **Win one tribe deeply (neighbor effect).** *"em um mundo polarizado, é mais valioso conquistar profundamente um grupo do que tentar agradar a todos"* (his reading of Jaguar's rebrand). Map influence networks ("quem influencia quem") instead of fighting bubbles head-on (blog, "A grama do vizinho...", December 2024).

- **Differentiation over benchmarking: the "gráfico da diferença."** Chasing a competitor's benchmark caps you at *equal*, never better. *Worked examples:* borrowing Harvard's Youngme Moon ("Different"), he charts a brand's capabilities against a flat market benchmark; for Reserva he scores logistics 5, creation 10, e-commerce 8, and says rebel brands ignore what sits below the line, double down on the peak, and are then forced to lift the rest to serve the demand it creates: *"O mercado não consegue explicar o que é diferente. Então eles dão um nome pro que é diferente de rebelde."* [The market can't explain what is different, so it calls it rebel.] (Marcas Rebeldes #01, unofficial captions). In writing he names Nizan, Renata Vichy, Abilio Diniz, Luiza Helena Trajano, Jobs, Beyoncé, Bezos, Elon, Taylor Swift, and, in an August 2023 post written before the split, **"os Birmans"**, as people who "nunca se preocuparam mais com o que os outros estavam fazendo, do que com o que eles próprios poderiam fazer" (blog, "Seja você mesmo, mas nem sempre o mesmo").
- **"Quatro olhos": watch the customer, not the rival.** The companion fable to the difference chart makes the same point through two lifelong-rival booksellers: the one who spent decades copying the other never accumulated the "four eyes" (his own attention *plus* the copier's) that customer-obsession generates, *"Eles sempre poderão copiar o que você já fez, mas jamais poderão copiar o que você irá fazer."*

- **Finite game vs. infinite game (imported from Simon Sinek).** Companies playing a "finite game" optimize the quarter; "infinite" ones compete against themselves for a cause that never ends. *Worked examples, three separate applications over three years:* Pope Francis's 2013 answer to a Church identity crisis, *"Fé e peregrinação"*, read as an "infinite leader" move (blog, "O papa é pop," 2022); Nike's "Winning isn't for everyone" campaign and its ad-question *"Am I a Bad Person?"* as a brand willing to alienate to stay coherent (blog, "Ser diferente é ser de verdade," 2024); and a binary "infinite leader vs. finite leader" grid (delegation vs. centralization, purpose vs. profit-only) built explicitly off Sinek plus Jack Welch's self-description as a CVO, *"Chief Vassoura Officer"* [Chief Broom Officer] (blog, "Precisamos falar de Céline Dion," using Céline Dion's illness-defying resilience as the frame).

- **Three phases of a Brazilian entrepreneurial cycle, and why founders become the bottleneck.** Phase 1: "você e teu sócio e Deus", door-to-door selling toward market fit. Phase 2: the founder must become a people-leader and encode knowledge into systems "sem engessar" the culture. Phase 3: *"Quando você foi bem sucedido nisso, o negócio cresceu, aí você começa a atrapalhar [...] a boca do funil é maior do que a que sai do outro lado e o pessoal engargala"* [you start getting in the way; the funnel's mouth outpaces what comes out the other end]. He frames his own 2024 exit as a self-diagnosed arrival at Phase 3, not a forced departure (TalksbyLeo #238).

- **"Caminho longo porém curto"** [the long-but-short road]. A rabbi's parable: a traveler is offered a short-but-long road (fast start, impassable obstacle) or a long-but-short one (slow, but it actually arrives); Meisler uses it to argue Brazil's "jeitinho" culture is the short-but-long road. *Worked example:* his own 18-year, self-funded build of Reserva, *"Sem atalho. Sem jeitinho. Sem fórmula mágica [...] Isso é ter cabeça de dono, não de apostador"* [No shortcuts. No magic formula. That's an owner's mindset, not a gambler's], versus *"os gurus de internet que empacotam o caminho curto como se ele fosse possível. E não é."* [internet gurus who package the short road as if it were possible] (LinkedIn Pulse, "Você está escolhendo o caminho errado," 2026). Spoken, he is harsher: *"vendedor de ilusão, né, que fica vendendo conversinha para quem já tá desesperado. Isso aí é sacanagem, né? É um crime, né?"* [illusion sellers peddling patter to people who are already desperate; that is a scam, a crime] (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions). This is the frame for his explicit disagreement with Beto Sicupira (see *Where they disagree*).

- **"Roube um estilo de liderança": curate, don't reinvent.** He names eight borrowed leadership lessons rather than claiming originality: Jack Welch's CVO (remove obstacles); Abílio Diniz's proximity-to-the-customer over hierarchy; his self-described *"madrinha"* [godmother] **Luiza Helena Trajano**, *"As pessoas não esperam que você seja perfeito, elas esperam que você seja honesto [...] nunca tentou ser perfeita e sim sempre buscou aprender com os erros"*; ex-Renault/Nissan/Ghosn executive Nélio Bilate on processes that must stay adaptable; Patagonia's Yvon Chouinard on why-over-what; volleyball coach Bernardinho's humility to "change himself, not the team"; his father **Luiz Meisler**'s "forget the spreadsheet, go sell door to door with humility"; and Unilever's Paul Polman dropping quarterly guidance (shares fell 8% on the announcement, then rose "mais de 290%" during his tenure) (blog, "Roube um estilo de liderança").

- **Company-as-bird / generational handoff, now with exit mechanics.** *"O empreendedor é como um pássaro: nasceu para voar..."* (blog, "Feliz Vida Nova!"). The mechanics behind that framing, in his own words: his father's warning that sellers who don't *understand* they sold "continuam querendo agir como donas" of a company that's no longer theirs; a self-described *"soube parar"* [we knew when to stop] read straight from football fandom ("você vê na bola é muito rápido você ver isso [...] o cara que sabe parar e o cara que não sabe parar"); and the line that closes the loop, *"Cumpri o ciclo"* (TalksbyLeo #238). Pairs with his exit pride at Sicomércio 2025: *"É muito raro um empreendedor que vive todas essas etapas e sai pela porta da frente."*

- **Daily operating system (102030 + MARTE).** Personal scaffolding: 10 min meditation / 20 pages / 30 min exercise; mornings for creative work; social-media blackout during work hours (blog, "O presente é o passado do futuro"). The personal-finitude version of the same instinct: *"Assumindo que possuo uma vida privilegiada [...] já vivi quase 50% do tempo que eu terei de vida"* and a proposed personal "north star objective" for *"LX" (life experience)*, on par with a business's CX (blog, "Pura vida").
- **The routine's failure mode, named.** *Worked example of the cost of ignoring it:* he names the pattern's failure directly, *"eu já tive cinco, seis burnouts na vida [...] eu tenho essa tendência, eu sou muito ansioso"* and, separately, years of avoidable fatigue from an undiagnosed vision problem he didn't fix out of vanity/neglect (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions). Fit Talk #01 (FitFeed's podcast) is built around the same turn; the host's description says he talks about discipline, mental health and "o momento em que entendeu que o sucesso sem bem-estar cobra caro" [the moment he understood that success without well-being exacts a price] (host text, not his words). The antidote he offers: *"a humildade é uma plantinha que a gente tem que regar todos os dias"*, followed at once by the jab at founders who flaunt wealth (see *Don't brag online* under *Decision heuristics*).

- **Brand-as-purpose ("marca com propósito") / 1P=5P.** The brand exists to advance a cause; the product funds and signals it. *Worked example:* after a 2014 trip to Pentecoste (Ceará), a young man told him *"Aqui na região nunca faltou escola, mas eu ia lá mesmo para comer"*, a *"surra moral"* [moral beating] that led to **Reserva 1p5p** (2016; each item funds five donated meals). NeoFeed puts the count at **100 million plates by August 2023** (20 million pieces sold); later counts run from "mais de 50 milhões" to "mais de 157 milhões de refeições" and do not reconcile (see *Provenance notes*); cite the specific source and date rather than averaging. He frames the private-sector role bluntly: *"Quando a iniciativa privada se coloca entre o problema e o cidadão, o bem prospera"* (Sicomércio 2025), and directly names the doctrine he's rejecting: see *Friedman* in *Where they disagree.*

- **Cash-first sequencing: wholesale makes the cash, retail makes the margin.** Rule: a fashion brand should start in wholesale because stock is financed at high interest (his figures: about 15% in Brazil against 5% in the US) while the P&L looks healthy. *Worked example, his own start:* the spreadsheets said opening one store would break them (outsourced minimum runs of 200 to 300 pieces per item), so he and Fernando sold to multi-brand stores first (about 80 to 90 doors before the first Reserva store), financed stores from wholesale cash and ran weekly cash targets. A second case: FARM going abroad through Anthropologie and trade fairs before opening small stores, which he calls his best bet for "a primeira grande história da moda brasileira" (Café com Ferri; ROI Hunters #349; both unofficial captions). *"Varejo não quebra no derré, varejo quebra no caixa."* (ROI Hunters, caption wording).
- **Inverted pyramid: the salesperson is the real boss.** If the customer is the boss, the person closest to the customer is the most senior role. *"Temos que inverter essa pirâmide e criar a cultura de que quem toca a companhia é o vendedor, é ele quem é o verdadeiro chefe"* [We must invert this pyramid and build a culture in which the salesperson runs the company] (PANROTAS 2025, reporter-attributed). *Worked example:* the store programme *"Movendo o Céu e a Terra pelo cliente"* paid the top commission to *"quem entregasse a melhor história de encantamento pro cliente e não a maior venda"* [whoever delivered the best customer-delight story, not the biggest sale] (Futurum Talks #56, unofficial captions). In Excepcionais he adds that the creative director must listen to the salesperson.
- **Training manuals as collective memory.** *"Na minha empresa, todos os manuais de treinamento são feitos com base no que todas as pessoas que passaram pela companhia fizeram. O jeito de entender evolui com o tempo. Experiência é gente"* [In my company all training manuals are built from what everyone who passed through the company did... Experience is people] (PANROTAS 2025, reporter-attributed). He later turned the same habit, writing case studies of books he read for his staff, into the format of the Manual de Donos newsletter (Futurum Talks, unofficial captions).
- **Seven-day relationship call, the Reservado bag, and the salesperson CRM.** A non-selling call a week after purchase ("Quem não é visto não é lembrado") brought the customer back, in his figures, with 97% probability within 30 days (Café com Ferri) or *"97,5%"* (Made in Brasil #174); the second purchase then "retains" the customer for 12 months (his rule, no data shown). *Worked examples:* a salesperson CRM ("AAL") that gives idle staff a call list, and "Reservado," a bag algorithmically composed (item count, top/bottom/accessory ratio, colours) and delivered home in 3 days, born from *"o cliente às vezes ele quer o produto, mas ele não consegue vir na loja"* [sometimes the customer wants the product but can't come to the store]. Claimed result: purchase frequency of almost six times a year against two for menswear. All self-reported, unofficial captions.
- **Store as the cheapest acquisition channel; internet as the single stock and the retention layer.** *"o físico é a melhor ferramenta de aquisição de cliente do mundo nessa nova jornada de consumo"* [the physical store is the best customer-acquisition tool in the world] and *"As DNVBs elas dependem do maior intermediário do mundo, o Google."* (Made in Brasil #174, unofficial captions). His arithmetic: mall rent around 12% of revenue against 20 to 30% to Google and Meta. *Worked example:* Reserva waited until 2012 for e-commerce because it first needed one consolidated stock and a self-built warehouse system; on the day it went live, physical-store sales rose 25% from stock availability alone, with no digital marketing: *"a internet é o canal logístico. A internet são todas as lojas. A internet é a visão de estoque único."*
- **"Somos Todos Vendedores."** Made in Brasil #174 gives 2024 revenue of about R$2B, of which 68% digital or digitally influenced, half of store-team commission earned online, and more than 2,500 salespeople trained as creators (editing, WhatsApp store, Instagram store); some sellers bill R$300,000 to R$400,000 a month without being in a store. *"Não existe o on e o off, existe o cliente e jornada."* [There is no online and offline, there is the customer and the journey.] Self-reported, unofficial captions.
- **Zona de potência (power zone) against the leader's benchmark.** *"Foca na zona de potência."* Owners fixate on gaps against the market leader, which shrinks their strengths; instead let strengths pull the weak side up. *Worked example, his own profile:* strong in communication and commercial, weak in financial management, managerial accounting and logistics, so he leaned on the first two. *"parar de olhar pro lado que um amiguinho é bom, porque seu amiguinho é bom não significa que você é bom"* (Futurum Talks #56, unofficial captions). Pairs with the *gráfico da diferença* above.
- **Ego is the failure mode of brand consolidation.** *"O ego é a coisa mais destrutiva que o negócio pode ter."* (Café com Ferri, unofficial captions). In Futurum Talks he says consolidation *"normalmente dá errado nesse setor, porque é muito ego"* [usually goes wrong in this sector because there is so much ego], with each brand's creative director believing they are the protagonist, and sets Reserva's counter-design against it: the customer is king, expressed in a 30m² first store with cold beer, house cappuccino and candy at the register before those became trends (unofficial captions). This is his own frame for the Azzas period; Azzas has not endorsed it.
- **Logo as shield; when the core customer outgrows the brand, build a separate one (Oficina Reserva).** *"A logo é um escudo"* (Café com Ferri) and *"A iconografia ela funciona como um escudo medieval."* (Entusiasta): logos signal membership and compensate insecurity, and confident consumers drift to "unbranded." *Worked example:* Reserva's customers aged and needed the brand less. A rack of upscale basics (Peruvian Pima cotton) inside Reserva stores went unseen amid the iconography, and Fernando teased him for wasting time; Rony insisted on another environment, a corner at Shopping Leblon with half a dozen items, and *"a loja explodiu, já nasceu explodindo porque o cliente já tinha falado que ele queria aquilo"* [the store exploded, it was born exploding because the customer had already said they wanted it]. Decision rule: *"não necessariamente a tua bolha representa todos os clientes."* (unofficial captions).
- **Scarcity before purchase, love after.** *"Antes de você virar cliente, ela vem da escassez. Depois que você vira cliente, ela vem do amor."* [Before you become a customer, it comes from scarcity; after, from love.] *Worked example:* *"Eu tenho uma capacidade de produção de 10 copos desse e eu tenho 100 pessoas querendo comprar esse copo. Quem eu vou privilegiar? Quem já comprou o meu copo antes"* [existing customers first]. Failure mode: a luxury brand that mistreats existing customers (he cites a friend's brand that required scheduling a call to repair a piece) (Entusiasta, unofficial captions).
- **Experience that outruns the price.** The business he looks for is *"Um negócio que tem uma experiência muito maior do que o valor real"* [a business whose experience is much greater than its real value], so any price feels reasonable, and *"A experiência é o produto e o produto é a experiência."* Companion rule from the book "Peak": do the basics before customization, since offering custom clothing is pointless in a store with no air conditioning (Entusiasta; Made in Brasil #174, unofficial captions).
- **Brand is built in time: the camel, not the unicorn.** *"Eu prefiro correr maratona do que ser 100 m rasos."* [I prefer to run a marathon than be a 100-metre sprinter.] and *"O camelo ele vai devagar, mas cara, ele não vai sentir sede não, velho."* (Made in Brasil #174). *Worked example, his reading:* Hermès overtook Louis Vuitton in market value (his figures, EUR 250B against 246B) by staying with quality, vertical production, scarcity and about 6 to 7% annual growth. In Startups podcast he adds that a Hermès bag at R$300,000 could not be sold at Zara, and that brand is built over time with "variáveis nativas humanas" (palate, smell, sight), which is why he argues taste cannot be fabricated by AI (unofficial captions; asserted, not evidenced).
- **Personal brand as algorithm arbitrage.** The platforms monetize company ad accounts, so a company page pays to be seen while a founder's profile gets organic reach: *"eu preciso ter o Rone porque eu vou gastar menos dinheiro para ter mais divulgação"* [I need to have Rony because I will spend less money to get more reach] (Made in Brasil #174; a CEO colleague told him a CEO should not give opinions, and he did anyway). *"o futuro da marca pessoal ele é a comunidade"* [the future of the personal brand is the community] (Startups podcast). *"Eu não sou influenciador, eu sou empreendedor rodando a CP."* (Café com Ferri; the caption reads "CP," read here as ACP). Unofficial captions.
- **ACP as his own operating system after Azzas.** He posted about studies, tech and brand, trained his algorithm, and DMs became deals: a gym (The Simple Gym), a creatine brand, a personal-brand agency (Blank), the newsletter *"E-mail do Rony"* and the paid school Manual de Donos (R$197 a month; a case on Tuesday, an AI case on Thursday, one live class a month; text built to be read in 20 minutes). Claimed scale, in July 2026: *"mais de 2 milhões de assinantes"* with a 40% open rate (Café com Ferri) against 2.8 million in June (Futurum Talks), and *"6.000 alunos"* in 40 days. Newsletter origin: a pandemic WhatsApp link list, banned, moved to email in 2021. All self-reported, unofficial captions.
- **WhatsApp groups as the community layer (G200, G50, G100, G300).** A group of the 200 best repeat customers is asked questions (store decoration, collection theme, campaign face) and pruned yearly; B2B groups of 50, 100 and 300 get different content. *Worked example:* a Carnaval 2020 "contatinho" shirt printed on demand with each buyer's own Instagram handle sold about 10,000 in three or four days and, in his words, *"salvou a gente na pandemia"* [saved us in the pandemic] (ROI Hunters #349, unofficial captions).
- **ACP applied by a third party: Dane-se.** *"Ele partiu do mesmo funil que usamos na Reserva. Primeiro, criou audiência. Depois, a comunidade e, com ela, o produto"* and *"E quando for lançado, a incerteza é muito baixa, porque a própria comunidade desenvolveu e é consumidora do produto. E são milhares de pessoas."* (NeoFeed, Nov 2025). *Worked example:* founder Gregório Machado posted about a mineral water in a can he did not yet have, listed it for sale with orders auto-cancelled to test demand, grew from 60 thousand to almost 330 thousand followers, let the community vote flavours and design, and sold out a first lot of 3,000 cans in hours; the energy-drink launch on 18 November 2025 carried over 1 million cans (NeoFeed's reporting).
- **The prank that becomes a lead.** On 1 April 2024 Rony posted an ironic April Fools piece about "branding muito chato" in water brands, announcing his own "Mais uma água" (NeoFeed's words); Machado took it seriously and wrote to him, which started the Dane-se relationship. A second case is the Doing Nothing Club (next bullet). Pattern: his jokes seed audience and deals, which sits uneasily with the authenticity thesis (see *Blind spots*).
- **Doing Nothing Club and the shelf-gap test.** Flooded with what-will-you-do-next calls and, he says, nobody asking if he was fine, he decided *"Eu vou fingir que eu vou lançar um clube."* [I will pretend to launch a club.] and filmed a bathrobe launch video (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions). He puts it at about 30,000 followers in a week; Exame, an outside account, called it an account created "numa mistura de provocação e autodeboche" with 16,000 followers in May 2025. The signal he read: a mental-health need, and a shelf gap (in his example, 70 natural-energy options at a US Whole Foods against three unhealthy ones in a Brazilian supermarket), the same logic as the five men in identical shorts. Rule: when you spot a gap while shopping abroad, test it.

- **Rebels selection test: founders who don't need you, and construction, not hype.** *"A gente gosta, como investidores, de encontrar pessoas que não precisam da gente como investidor, que se sustentam sozinhas"* [As investors we like to find people who don't need us as investors, who sustain themselves] (Exame, Oct 2026). *Worked example, The Simple Gym:* *"A Simple tem caixa, tem lucro, tem fit com o mercado. Não é hype. É construção"*, positioned in a gap, *"Existe um buraco entre as academias populares e os clubes premium. E é nesse meio que a Simple se posiciona"*, by founders with *"um amor quase doentio pelo que fazem"* [an almost sick love for what they do] (Exame, May 2025). In his words the pull was the brand: *"A The Simple Gym chamou nossa atenção pelo Instagram... Mandamos direct e, de repente, estávamos conversando com os fundadores. Foi amor à primeira vista"* (Fitfeed, June 2025).
- **Rebels' three values, used at the fork.** The first filter is an obsessive entrepreneur; the values test is three carried over from Reserva with the same partners: a dream that is "bom e grande," *"mente aberta"* (innovation's raw material is *audição*), and a commitment to people as much as results. Why only three: *"O valor serve na hora da bifurcação."* [Values serve at the fork in the road.] Lists of 10 or 15 get forgotten (TalksbyLeo #238). Partner choice is *"primeiro quem, depois como"* [first who, then how] (Marcas Rebeldes #01) and a marriage: "montanha, não é praia e montanha" (ROI Hunters #349). All unofficial captions.
- **Rebels' sourcing funnel and diligence rhythm.** He planned a vibe-coded landing page for 60 to 100 leads and received about 1,500 in May 2025; the partners split them and held six or seven 1 to 1.5 hour conversations a day for three to four months, transcribing every call into a vector-searchable "segundo cérebro do fundo" (TalksbyLeo #238). By his June 2026 count: *"a gente conversou com mais de 400 empresas nesse ano"* and *"a gente fez 10 negócios nesse ano"*, with a fifth partner, a 22-year-old named Michel (Entusiasta). Before investing the partners offer a three to six month mentorship; the launch coverage quotes *"Vamos investir em poucos, no máximo dez. Faremos uma mentoria de três a seis meses antes de decidir"* (Guia JeansWear). Unofficial captions except the last.
- **Rebels' operating mode: copilot, quiet in the room, 20% adoption.** *"Somos copilotos. Não estamos aqui para mandar, mas para construir junto."* (Exame, May 2025; the page gives the line no speaker tag, but it follows a quote attributed to Meisler and a reporter's sentence, so read it as Rony speaking for Rebels, probably). In results meetings *"a gente vai ficar lá quietinho"* and gives feedback after; *"com muita sorte, em 20% dos casos que a gente opina eles escutam"* [with luck, in 20% of cases where we opine, they listen], which he calls *"monstruosamente alta"* (a high rate) because as an entrepreneur he would not have listened to three (Excepcionais, unofficial captions). The hiring lesson of the first operation: Gustavo Lerner, almost 25 years in Globo's commercial area, hired for revenue at Canal Foco (Exame, Oct 2026).
- **Rebels' economics and exit rule.** Club deal, own capital; in his July 2026 count: eight companies, R$78M invested ("100% grana própria"), portfolio valued at "100 a 120 milhões," mainly purchase options plus some convertibles, a pipeline of 15 to 20, a bi-weekly investment committee and monthly results committee (Excepcionais, unofficial captions). *"E não temos prazo de saída. Se um dia isso acontecer, será com o empreendedor. Estamos juntos com eles. Na saúde e na doença. Na alegria e na tristeza."* (NeoFeed, Nov 2025). In Futurum Talks he adds a rule of thumb: enter up to about R$50M revenue, help the jump to R$200M to R$300M, then recommend a sale to a strategic or fund because Brazil's continental scale demands capital then; skin in the game means he backs only founders who already put their own money in (unofficial captions).
- **Brand buys access: the Gooday case.** When Rebels came in (end of 2024 in ROI Hunters; Excepcionais ranks it second after Simple Gym), Rony told the founders *"esse negócio aqui tem mais marca que negócio"* [this business has more brand than business]: a year of consumer-brand firepower meant retail buyers already knew the name, so B2B would be easier by "10 degraus" (steps) instead of 100. Revenue as he gives it: about R$12M in 2024, about R$80M in 2025, and R$100M (ROI Hunters, May) then R$130 to 140M (Excepcionais, July/August) expected for 2026. On the deal itself: *"Não existe decisão nossa no nosso modelo de negócio, né?"* [There is no decision of ours in our business model.] Founder-reported, unofficial captions.
- **Blank: sell distribution, and let the founders decide if you deserve equity.** Blank began as a certification info-product that "não vai escalar," pivoted to a personal-brand agency, and Rony became its pilot client. When its founders refused a new partner ("pacto de sangue"), he offered to be a paying client first: *"se der certo ... vocês me dizem se eu mereço ser sócio ou não"* [if it works, you tell me whether I deserve to be a partner] (TalksbyLeo #238). Claimed size drifts: 90 personal brands and R$10M with a R$30M target (ROI Hunters, May) against 80 and R$15M to R$20M heading to R$45M (TalksbyLeo, June). Unofficial captions, founder-reported.
- **Media thesis: own attention with a brand.** *"Quem tem atenção faz todo o resto"* [whoever has attention does everything else later] (Excepcionais, unofficial captions). In October 2026 Rebels put an undisclosed sum into Canal Foco, a YouTube entertainment channel created in 2023 with R$50,000 whose main format puts one person against thirty in a debate; Rony joined its panel on "Os Caras do Pix" before the deal, and the rationale is its own audience as a distribution channel for new products (Exame). Its CEO's targets (R$25M to 30M in advertising in 2027, R$100M in 2028) are the channel's, not verified.
- **Selection by edge: Rebels is not a growth shop.** *"O meu modelo, o modelo de gestão que eu posso ajudar [...] não é growth, é que queima a caixa muitas vezes para crescer. [...] não é minha praia"* [the management model I can help with isn't growth, which often burns cash to grow; it isn't my thing] (TalksbyLeo #238), an explicit refusal to sell what he doesn't have; in Futurum Talks, *"eu não sei fazer essas teses malucas de growth, né? A, B, C, D."* and *"eu nunca verticalizei e nem pretendo verticalizar assim, porque eu acho que a indústria é um bicho totalmente diferente"* [I never verticalized and don't intend to, because industry is a totally different animal]. His zone is *"geração de valor, recriação de marca, é máquina de venda"* [value generation, brand recreation, a sales machine] (Startups.com.br summary). Unofficial captions where noted.
- **Build vs buy, and example over advice.** A rule reported by the newsletter author: if more than 15 off-the-shelf solutions already do the function, buy rather than build (Micro-SaaS newsletter, a third-party summary; the "15" is the author's formulation). Two lines attributed to him: *"conselho é bom, exemplo arrasta"* and *"se ideia valesse alguma coisa, era vendida a 10 centavos o balde"* [if an idea were worth anything, it would be sold at ten cents a bucket] (same source), the first also in Made in Brasil #174 as *"Se o conselho é bom, exemplo arrasta"* (unofficial captions).
- **Owner mode vs executive mode (the Iguatemi protocol).** He sits on the Iguatemi board and describes the owners' arrangement: three siblings left operations, a career executive is CEO, the siblings form the board, and each Friday they spend two hours with the responsible operator on one demand tied to each sibling's specialty (capital structure, mall mix, commercial). *"Isso é dono."* [That is an owner.] An owner sets strategy, allocates capital, mentors people and lives on dividends; an executive runs the machine (ROI Hunters #349, unofficial captions).
- **Founder mode, answered.** On Brian Chesky and "founder mode": *"Eu acho o Brian muito jovem."* and *"Eu acho que ele saiu no tempo errado."* (the company was too young and the step back weakened culture), but stepping back is justified once succession has produced a strong executive. His own lean is toward *"economia real"*: *"Eu sou um cara mais de economia real."* Elsewhere: *"Uma companhia tem que ter um dono, tem que ter alguém que decide."* (TalksbyLeo #238). Unofficial captions.
- **Leading upward and founder-to-executive rules.** After the sale he treated it as a craft: know you no longer own it; *"liderar para cima"*; put up the *"arame farpado"* so the acquirer's culture does not touch the team; rotate the burden among the four partners (when one tired, another went to war); deliver numbers because results buy freedom (Futurum Talks). He renamed his own routines to match Azzas governance (weekly sales meeting "COMEX," monthly results "N1") while running them his own way, kept two-year plans because *"4 anos não existe"* and called the leader *"o algodão entre os cristais"* (Excepcionais). *"O empreendedor é um bicho. O executivo é um bicho totalmente diferente, velho."* (Futurum Talks). Unofficial captions.
- **Innovation is phases of a project, not a department.** *"A inovação não é uma área de negócio, a inovação são fases de um projeto."* and *"A dificuldade é quando um projeto de fase um é tratado como projeto de fase três."* [The difficulty is when a phase-one project is treated as a phase-three project.] Applied to his own intrapreneurship at Azzas. Also: *"uma empresa é tão somente a sua capacidade de comunicação"* and *"Processo é tubo de encanamento da onde passa informação"* [process is the plumbing through which information flows] (Powercast #248, unofficial captions).
- **Persistence versus stupidity.** *"Errar é humano, né? Insistir num erro é burrice."* [To err is human; persisting in an error is stupidity.] Budget a margin for innovation and error inside the plan, and stop before becoming *"comandante naufrágio"* (Futurum Talks #56, unofficial captions). Pairs with *"Não tem como inovar se não errar"* (PANROTAS 2025).
- **Purpose welded to the payslip.** At Reserva the number of meals donated through 1P=5P appeared on each employee's pay stub: *"No contracheque do cara, vim a quantidade de refeição que o trabalho dele levou ele complementar."* (Startups podcast) and *"no contra-cheque da galera vem a quantidade de refeição per cápta"* (Excepcionais). His principle: *"agenda de sustentabilidade que é propulsora de valor é a que tá vinculada ao negócio"* [a sustainability agenda that drives value is the one tied to the business], because it creates belonging. Unofficial captions.
- **1P=5P origin and best campaign.** Rony went to meet Blake Mycoskie of Toms because he wanted to launch something similar, which led to 1P=5P; *"a melhor campanha de comunicação, de marketing e de marca que a gente já fez na nossa história foi o lançamento de um P5P"* [the best communication, marketing and brand campaign we ever made was the launch of 1P=5P]: before it almost nobody wrote to him about purchases, afterwards he got dozens of messages a week from customers leaving five, ten or twenty plates (Marcas Rebeldes #01, unofficial captions). Meal counts differ by source (see *Provenance notes*).
- **Brand elasticity (Virgin).** *"são marcas elásticas, né, maior parte dos casos, porque elas não estão muito preocupadas com as regras do jogo"* [they are elastic brands because they aren't much worried about the rules of the game], and *"Sir Richard Branson é, em minha opinião, o empreendedor rebelde mais admirável do mundo."* Branson licensing the Virgin brand into airlines, hotels and telephony as separate companies is, he says, what he dreams of having done with Reserva (Marcas Rebeldes #01, unofficial captions).
- **Reserva Inc: a customer request becomes a platform.** A customer asked a store to print a T-shirt for his *chopada*; no fashion company offered it, so Reserva imported an Israeli textile printer, piloted with orders in a notebook, built a self-service online editor with 7-working-day delivery, then opened it as a platform: *"mais de 120.000 logistas na plataforma"* (Made in Brasil #174) against "mais de 120 lojistas" (Marcas Rebeldes #01); the first looks right. Lesson: *"o branding é o modelo de negócio no mundo que a gente vive"*; brainstorm business models, not only products. Unofficial captions, self-reported.
- **Use one product to enter; do not communicate the whole range.** For a fashion founder: pick one hero product and communicate it ostensively until you are tired: *"quem tá cansado é você, não é o público"* [you are the one who is tired, not the public]. Reserva's counterexample: with 400 to 600 SKUs *"você tentava comunicar tudo, você não comunica nada"* (ROI Hunters #349, unofficial captions). In 2021 or 2022 it built its own YouTube channel "Geração R" with an unknown host and a survey-suggested humour format; "não foi."
- **Boring business plus a brand layer.** Menswear was a "boring business" lifted by brand and experience; Brazilian health and wellness is boring in its communication. He cites Codie Sanchez's roll-up playbook (buy cash-generating small businesses from retiring owners, put them under one brand) and adds a capital-structure twist: buy, then franchise to scale with less capital (ROI Hunters #349, unofficial captions; the roll-up references came up in the hosts' discussion).
- **Plan the commercial calendar backwards.** In his claim 97% of Brazilian entrepreneurs do not plan one. Method: plan the tip of the iceberg (the sales peaks) and slice backwards through the supply chain; used in a Manual de Donos live class on a 2026 calendar crowded by the World Cup, elections and holidays (Startups podcast, unofficial captions).
- **Zig when others zag, with diligence.** When everyone told him to launch mentoring, he chose a low-ticket paid newsletter: *"Tá todo mundo indo para um lugar. vai pro outro, não necessariamente porque o outro é um lugar maior, mas tá menos crowd"*, but *"Óbvio que você tem que ser diligente e pensar porque às vezes esse negócio é na morto, né?"*; low ticket was chosen because he wants a business that reaches millions: *"É melhor, mas não escala Brasil."* (ROI Hunters #349, on few clients paying a lot). Unofficial captions.
- **Manual de Donos and the four needs of the small owner.** Knowledge, credit, management technology and investment (Futurum Talks); *"o pequeno e médio empreendedor no Brasil tá exausto"* [the small and mid-sized entrepreneur in Brazil is exhausted] (ROI Hunters). He cites a Cabeça de Dono survey (Instituto Locomotiva with Itaú BBA, from his recall, not checked): over 40% exhausted, over 50% lonely, over 60% unable to do financial management, over 90% without quality family time (TalksbyLeo). The stated aim: *"a gente quer em 5 anos fazer 1 milhão de cabeças de donos"* (Startups podcast). A banking arm for entrepreneurs, with Stone, is the fourth piece (Powercast). Unofficial captions.
- **AI first by process, not by tool.** Map processes by repeated effort, need for a boss's approval and cost or time, automate the top cluster, then ask the model which tool. *"Pessoas pegam lá a senha da adapta, pô, distribuem pra companhia e acham que é a companhia AI driven. Assim, não tá tá com Google super turbinado."* (Café com Ferri). Personal case: his 10/20/30 routine plus an AI "coach chato" on WhatsApp at 7:10, which became the habit-coach product "Superdash": *"automatizando problemas da minha vida, eu criei soluções de negócio para os outros também"* (Made in Brasil #174). Why: *"Eu estudo porque eu tenho medo."* (Excepcionais). Unofficial captions.
- **The doorman fallacy and the premium on human service.** Borrowing Rory Sutherland: cutting the doorman to save salary removes the human experience that made the building feel like a hotel. His extension: as digital commerce automates, human service becomes scarce and priced higher (therapy with GPT against human therapists, whose average ticket "vai subir para caramba"); *"o in real life vai ser mais valorizado"*; Rebels therefore sees offline (the gym) as an investment area (Café com Ferri; Startups podcast, unofficial captions). A caution he gives to companies: be certain AI cannot do the job you are about to hire for (Powercast).
- **AI slop and the community exit.** *"a gente tá vivendo o ano 26, ao meu ver, é o ano do AI Slop em termos de conteúdo na internet"* (Startups podcast). The escape is community and the right 10,000 people over reach (TalksbyLeo #238); *"A gente trabalha de graça para elas todos os dias."* on the social platforms (Startups podcast). Dated predictions, unofficial captions.
- **Fashion shows as parody.** At São Paulo Fashion Week Reserva mocked the establishment: costumes bought in Saara (Shrek, Flintstones, Mickey) hung in the photographers' pit with a handwritten note inviting the front row to take one home, because this is not a show, it is a costume. The press reaction was negative the next day; customers noticed. In 2009 an uninvited television prank on the runway (a Pânico "Impostor") was bet on by the founders as TV, and gave a no-name brand national airtime (Futurum Talks #56, unofficial captions; the 2009 episode is described by the hosts).
- **Operating system updates.** *"eu acredito mais em sistema do que em meta"* [I believe more in systems than in goals] (Made in Brasil #174). Reading: *"eu leio quatro a cinco livros por mês desde que eu me conheço por gente"* (Startups podcast), two hours a day of phone use enforced with the app Opal, long-form content only. *"Humildade não é o exercício diário. Humildade é um exercício minutário, principalmente para nós empreendedores."* (Made in Brasil #174). Family pact: *"em quinto lugar o trabalho para dar orgulho pra família"* [fifth, work, to make the family proud] (Café com Ferri). Unofficial captions.
- **Record the imperfect start.** Film sales calls, supplier talks, rejections and defects; vulnerability builds an audience so CAC becomes *"ou zero ou negativo"* (Powercast #248). The Marcas Rebeldes logo is a scribble: *"imperfeição é valor"* [imperfection is value] (Marcas Rebeldes #01). Family rituals as management: at Reserva conventions, spouses and children sat in the front row when staff were honoured and he addressed the children from the stage (Powercast). Unofficial captions.
- **The spreadsheet confirms; the street decides (the first shorts).** At 23, still at Accenture and living with his parents, he and Fernando found a São Gonçalo factory through a CNPJ printed on a clothing label; asked for vector artwork, a spec sheet, Corel and Photoshop, they had none, so in the car they typed "Be Yourself" in Word (Arial) for the leg of the first shorts, a first order of about 300 pieces. Each put in R$3,000 (R$6,000 in total) and they became street peddlers. Their business-case spreadsheet projected a billion in revenue in three months (*"planilha papel e ouvido aceita tudo"* [spreadsheet, paper and ears accept anything]), his father told them to go out and sell, and its one real lesson was that opening a store first would break them on production minimums, so they started with multi-brand stores. The gym scene that started it: *"tinha uns cinco caras usando uma bermuda da mesma estampa mesma cor Idêntica"* (Os Sócios cut, unofficial captions).

## Decision heuristics

- **Don't wait for perfect conditions; test cheap, then scale capital.** "Tinta amarela": pilot on the worst store, let the result sign the check for the rest.
- **Listen before you sell; the raw material of innovation is audição, not tech.** Repeated across five years of sources, treat this as his default lens on *any* new problem.
- **Ask what a choice does to the community and the cause, not just the P&L**, in his framing the two converge over time.
- **Sequence for margin and emotion.** Put the high-margin, high-customization choice first.
- **Win one tribe deeply over pleasing everyone** (neighbor effect); differentiate rather than benchmark ("gráfico da diferença," "quatro olhos").
- **Hire for attitude/culture over pedigree;** two outsiders (an engineer, an ad exec) entered fashion on curiosity and courage, not credentials.
- **When something blows up, look for the bigger business inside the wreckage** (Use Huck → white-label, 10×).
- **Take the long-but-short road; distrust anyone selling a shortcut ("jeitinho," infoproduct gurus).**
- **Know when a phase has ended and step back, before the body forces you to.** His own checkup was the forcing function; the "3 phases" model is the retrospective explanation.
- **Steal shamelessly and cite your source.** He treats borrowing a leadership lesson from a named mentor as more credible than claiming originality.

- **Cash before P&L: start in wholesale, hold weekly cash meetings, ask where the cash breaks.** (Café com Ferri; ROI Hunters #349; unofficial captions)
- **Find the product for your customer, not the customer for your product.** *"então você acha produto pro teu cliente, não cliente pro teu produto"* (Café com Ferri); *"A gente tem que achar produto pro nosso cliente, não cliente pro nosso produto."* (Excepcionais). Unofficial captions.
- **Pick the name nobody loves or hates at first, and not your own.** He chose a name that "a princípio não fede nem cheiro" [at first neither stinks nor smells] so polarization does not slow patient construction, and not a founder's name so the brand can outlive him: designers named after themselves (Lauren, Valentino, Armani) could not age (Café com Ferri, unofficial captions).
- **Hire for technique and values; recruit more than you train.** *"As pessoas falam muito de treinamento e pouco de recrutamento."* and *"Gente boa, você não treina ninguém."* (ROI Hunters #349; Excepcionais). In theory the order should be reversed: *"A gente deveria contratar com dificuldade e demitir com facilidade, certo?"*, which Latin founders rarely do (Made in Brasil #174). Unofficial captions.
- **Say no more than yes.** *"saber dizer não é mais importante que sim"* and *"As pessoas confundem quantidade de informação com quantidade de atenção."* (Café com Ferri, unofficial captions).
- **Test the hobby that earns money when you should be resting.** *"se você faz de maneira remunerada, quando você deveria estar descansando, é esse treco aí que você deveria empreender"* (Café com Ferri, unofficial captions).
- **When friends say it does not exist, take it as a signal.** *"Ninguém bom tenta."* [No good person tries.]: "low touch não existe" and "homem não compra roupa" were friendly objections that made others give up, and he built low-touch education and Reserva anyway (Startups podcast, unofficial captions).
- **Do not go into debt for a passion.** *"Não dê passo marca a perna."* (the caption garbles the saying about a step longer than the leg; Entusiasta, unofficial captions).
- **Ask who owns your time.** *"Eu nunca trabalhei tão pouco na vida, mas eu nunca entreguei tanto valor na vida."* and *"a liberdade boa é a liberdade controlada"* (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions).
- **Don't brag online.** On founders showing cars and watches right after their first money: *"isso aí é o beijo da morte"*; *"os caras melhor sucedido que eu conheço na minha vida nem usam relógio"* (TalksbyLeo #238; Entusiasta, unofficial captions). Mind the tension noted in *Blind spots*.
- **Don't talk about the company you left while the successors are building it.** He declined podcast invitations for about a year "até por respeito" and spoke when he had a new story (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions).
- **Leave a stage when the future no longer excites you, with succession ready, through the front door.** *"Cara, não me empolga mais futuro aqui."* (Futurum Talks #56, unofficial captions).
- **Keep luck in perspective.** *"não vamos começar a acreditar que a gente é Midas"* [let's not start believing we are Midas] (Excepcionais, unofficial captions).

## Positions by decision type

- **Pivot vs. persevere.** Persevere on the *brand/cause*, pivot the *vehicle*. The Use Huck failure was re-pointed into a white-label service that dwarfed the original deal. When a two-year fashion non-compete from the Azzas exit blocked him from re-entering apparel, he didn't fight the clause or wait it out, he pivoted Rebels Ventures entirely into wellness/longevity, a sector the non-compete didn't reach (NeoFeed, Rebels exclusive).
- **How to price / where margin lives.** Treat CX and customization as variable-cost margin levers (blog, "A ordem dos fatores...").
- **When/how to validate before committing capital.** Run the smallest, cheapest version of the test on the *worst* asset first; let the pilot's number authorize the full spend ("Tinta amarela").
- **When to sell / exit.** Position: sell from strength, take governance seriously while you're in, and know the difference between *knowing* you sold and *understanding* you sold (his father's distinction). He sold a profitable, growing brand on a market-sizing argument, stayed four years, which he calls an "MBA" in running a public company, and left when his own three-phase model said he had become the bottleneck, with the health crisis as the forcing function. The deal terms, his reasons and the press account follow below.
- **Raise vs. bootstrap.** Reserva ran on R$6,000 total invested capital, ever, and he describes it as profitable throughout: *"Fomos lucrativos em todos os 18 anos que estivemos à frente do negócio."* (LinkedIn Pulse, "Você está escolhendo o caminho errado," 2026), though a spoken account from the same year says *"foram 17 anos de lucro e caixa"* with a loss only in 2020, and a very small one (Powercast #248, unofficial captions; see *Provenance notes*). Rebels Ventures is capitalized the same way at the fund level: no outside LPs, a "club deal" where each partner sizes their own check per deal.
- **Who to bring in as partners.** Explicitly modeled on 3G Capital's Beto Sicupira / Marcel Telles / Jorge Paulo Lemann, long-term, inseparable partnership as the template for both the original Reserva founding team and the four-person Rebels Ventures split (Rony = strategy/brand; Fernando Sigal = supply chain; Jayme Nigri Moszkowicz = operations; José Alberto Silva = digital).
- **Focus vs. diversify.** Diversify *within* a coherent brand universe (Reserva Mini/Go/Ink, Eva, Oficina) rather than scattering; for the *country*, Brazilian brands must dominate the continental home market before going global (NeoFeed, global-brand piece).
- **Build for one segment vs. mass appeal.** Win one tribe deeply; depth beats breadth (blog, "A grama do vizinho...").
- **Founder vs. corporate-parent control.** His own case is the cautionary position, a creative, decentralizing founder under a centralizing controller is a structural mismatch: *"O estilo mais centralizador de Birman também bateu de frente com a personalidade de Rony"* (Exame). Complication: he says he *tried* to internalize his father's advice to respect the new owner's authority for four years before concluding the fit had run out, the friction wasn't instant refusal, it was a slow-building mismatch.
- **How to monetize expertise honestly.** Package what you know as a cheap, practical "manual," not an infoproduct funnel or a course sold at guru prices, an explicit contrast with the "vendedor de ilusão" who sells "conversinha para quem já tá desesperado" (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions) and with the gurus of the LinkedIn Pulse piece "Você está escolhendo o caminho errado". The same piece doubles as the launch funnel for his own paid school (see *Blind spots*).
- **Terms of the 2020 sale: governance before price, stock-linked consideration, no earn-out.** Preparing the IPO he read Chris Voss and treated the sale as a game. Conditions came first: staying in Rio with autonomy and governance meetings; *"se a gente não chegasse num acordo de como seria a nossa vida depois, não teria preço que pagasse"* [if we hadn't agreed how our life would be afterwards, no price would have paid]. He and the partners set a minimum price, and *"Eu vendi pelo dobro."* [I sold for double.] Payment was partly Arezzo shares: *"tudo cash out, né? Mas 20% à vista e os outros em lockups que terminavam só no final do quarto ano"* (Startups podcast); he chose not to have an earn-out (the caption garbles the word) and says the governance clauses effectively dissolve because you don't sue over governance contracts. *"O nosso salário não aumentou depois da venda."* (Excepcionais). Unofficial captions.
- **Why he sold: pandemic liquidity, not a plan to exit.** Stores closed on 18 March 2020, with three months of cash for almost 3,000 people and a decision not to lay anyone off (*"A gente tomou decisão de não demitir."*); his leverage of about half an EBITDA turn turned from an advantage into a disadvantage; as a father of three, after being close to death by his account, he wanted some liquidity: *"tirar algumas fichas da mesa"* [take some chips off the table]. A syndicate bank pitched a fast track to the stock market through Arezzo. By 18 June 2020 sales were growing 20% with all stores closed, digital only: *"A gente pousou o avião no rio Hudson"* (Powercast #248; ROI Hunters #349; unofficial captions). The principle he states: *"Se você construir uma empresa assim, e caso apareça alguém querendo comprar ela, ela vai valer mais."* [Build a company you'd enjoy forever; if someone wants to buy it, it will be worth more.]
- **The father's two tests and the Harvard statistic.** Before signing, his father asked whether he was sure he wanted to sell and then said *"se quiser vender, venda. venda."* A friend sent a Harvard paper: *"Em 1% dos casos depois de 4 anos, a empresa existe."* and in about 2% of those the founder is still there (he fumbles the numbers on air: "Minto"; no citation, unverified). His reaction: *"Deus existe mas não faz milagre."* [God exists but doesn't do miracles.] Odds favour being out within four years, hence *"devolver"* value to the buyer who paid (Startups podcast; ROI Hunters #349; Powercast #248; unofficial captions).
- **Selling a company is selling your child.** *"Você não tá vendendo uma coisa, você tá vendendo o teu filho."* [You aren't selling a thing, you're selling your child.] and *"O apartamento vale quanto um quer pagar e o outro quer receber."* [The apartment is worth what one wants to pay and the other to receive.] His advice on valuation and process, with the pull to keep acting as owner: *"Eu acho que o grande problema do empreendedor quando ele vende uma companhia é que ele ele vende, mas ele acha que ele não vendeu, que ele vai continuar dono."* (Excepcionais; Futurum Talks #56; unofficial captions).
- **His own account of the exit (all unofficial captions).** He told Alexandre Birman of the decision at the end of 2023 or in January 2024 (Powercast: 2 or 3 January), with a transition of about eight months to August 2024; Birman wanted a market CEO (Ruy Kameyama, per Bloomberg Línea), with eight or nine directors (his counts differ) left in place, none with much more than ten years in a company 18 years old. He told his four partners first: *"Eu tô indo. Se vocês quiserem, turma, fiquem. Para mim fechou."* and they chose to leave with him.
- **Why he says he left (all unofficial captions).** The reason he gives is not money (same salary, no earn-out) but a consolidation vision *"que a gente em parte concordava, em parte discordava"* (Futurum Talks), *"Era só um lugar de mundo que a gente não concordava tanto"* (Startups podcast), time ownership (see *Core ideas*), and burnout from politics: *"O motivo do cansaço foi toda a questão política e da governança e da construção de PowerPoints e da construção de Excel"* [the reason for the exhaustion was all the politics and governance and building PowerPoints and Excel files] (Excepcionais). On conflict: *"Nunca teve briga. Zero."* (ROI Hunters), *"Briga, nunca teve briga nenhuma, zero."* (Powercast). Weigh against the press accounts in *Blind spots*.
- **A rare, hedged counterfactual on Alexandre Birman.** *"se eu pudesse voltar no tempo, eu sugeriria o Alexandre, né? Primeira vez que eu falo isso, eh, que o Alexandre fosse virasse dono"* [if I could go back in time I would suggest that Alexandre become an owner; first time I say this]: using Benchimol's line *"tem uma hora que a cadeira do CEO fica pequeno pro dono"* (he attributes it to Guilherme Benchimol) and Bezos's inbox-outbox anecdote (Excepcionais; Powercast, unofficial captions). In Café com Ferri he calls what is happening at the company he left *"um papo de maluco de ego, né? de vaidade"*, an interested characterization he did not support with specifics, while praising Anderson Birman's model, not Alexandre's, as "Zara do calçado" (unofficial captions).
- **What the press and analysts said about the exit, attributed.** Bloomberg Línea (29 Aug 2024): Ruy Kameyama became CEO of the unit; co-founders Fernando Sigal, Jayme Nigri Moszkowicz and José Alberto da Silva left too; Azzas cited only the end of their 2020 employment contracts; shares were down near 5% early that afternoon. Itaú BBA's Thiago Macruz: *"Vemos Rony como um executivo excepcional, mas, ao mesmo tempo, notamos que o segundo nível de executivos da Reserva continua na empresa."* XP's team (Danniela Eiger) read the timing as the executives' contracts ending with their last lock-up tranche: *"Observamos que o fim do ciclo dos executivos coincide com sua última parcela de lock-up"*. Both are analysts' readings, not statements by Rony, who posted "As nove verdades dolorosas da vida" on LinkedIn a week earlier without mentioning Azzas (*"Tenha cuidado com quem você confia. Sal e açúcar parecem iguais"*); any link to the exit is the reader's inference.
- **After he left, what Azzas said (context, not causation).** In NeoFeed's coverage of Azzas's 2025 results, Alexandre Birman called 2025 *"um ano de consolidação na profissionalização da gestão"* in the men's unit (Reserva), with a plan to grow full-price sales and cut entry-price, lower-margin products: *"E conseguimos"*; fashion men revenue was R$1.94B, up 7.4%, and Azzas was considering spinning the unit off (NeoFeed). These are Birman's and the CFO's statements about the company Rony left; the dossier draws no causal line to him.
- **Fashion non-compete and a return to fashion.** Analysts told Bloomberg Línea of a two-year clause from December 2024 covering menswear and related segments; the Rebels launch coverage says the same (Guia JeansWear). Rony says it ends on 4 December; fashion would suit one of two Rebels horizontals (consumer brands) but conflicts with its risk appetite because of size grids and seasonality. On buying Reserva back: *"obviamente não pelo mesmo preço que tu vendeu"* and *"dizer nunca é complicado"* (Café com Ferri, unofficial captions).
- **Dividends: the founder's regret.** Asked whether he took dividends at Reserva, he answers *"Tirava nada"* [I took nothing out]; the company reinvested, opening 40 to 50 stores a year; today he says he would have opened 20 to 25, paid dividends and taken no debt (*"Não, não teria feito dívida. Teria aberto menos, teria crescido menos rápido."*). He criticizes Silicon Valley dogma that "quem distribui dividendo é porque não sabe reinvestir," which he says funds sold because they want growth then exit, and cites Snap's Evan Spiegel's secondary sale as the case where founder liquidity let him refuse Meta. At Rebels he tells partners to distribute and says Blank and Gooday paid dividends last year; four of ten investments did not in 2025 (ROI Hunters #349; Excepcionais; unofficial captions). Made in Brasil #174 has a line that the group "sempre pagou, reinvestiu, distribuiu", probably referring to a different period: unresolved.
- **Public company or private?** After four years inside a public company: *"eu não teria empresa pública. Eu teria sempre empresa fechada que distribui dividendo e que enriquece os sócios e e os colaboradores."* (his hedge: "nunca diga nunca") (ROI Hunters #349, unofficial captions). Results are the only protection: *"Mas o dia que a gente não der resultado, vão mexer com a gente e tem que mexer"* (Powercast #248).
- **Do not verticalize into manufacturing; source at home on conviction.** Stay in brand and sales and find a product with enough margin to pay the factory. In the pandemic Reserva kept its Brazilian suppliers, who in his telling refused to give factory space to competitors: *"esses caras nos ajudaram quando ninguém, quando vocês nem olhavam pra gente"*; *"Algumas vezes a planilha explicava, outras vezes era só convicção."* and *"Não foi o cara mais genial que não quebrou na pandemia, foi o cara que não parou."* because the fashion chain starts 18 months ahead (Futurum Talks #56; Excepcionais; unofficial captions).
- **Rebels' revenue band and size, as stated at different dates.** Launch coverage (NeoFeed's Rebels exclusive and Guia JeansWear): bootstrapped companies billing R$1M to R$20M, average stake about 20%. Exame (May 2025): R$1M to R$20M. In TalksbyLeo #238 (captions) he says businesses "que faturam de 5 a 50 milhões, que já tenham market fit e que sejam geradores de lucro e caixa". NeoFeed (Nov 2025, reporter's paraphrase): a floor of R$20M, stakes of 15 to 30%, checks "em geral, entre R$ 1 milhão e R$ 5 milhões," and a new "Rebel Labs" arm for smaller businesses when the founder and brand are special (*"Vamos fazer negócios menores quando entendermos que o empreendedor e a marca são especiais"*). Rony's own later figures: R$3M to R$30M (Café com Ferri), R$3M to R$50M (Excepcionais, most recent). Do not average; see *Provenance notes*.
- **Advice to a founder pitching Rebels, and to a beginner.** *"Antes de começar não nos procura."* and *"Aqui, ó, a coisa mais difícil é começar."*; in the first year *"Quanto mais você conseguir fazer de caixa e lucro nesse começo, melhor"*; *"Só a venda salva."* (ROI Hunters #349; Powercast #248; unofficial captions). On Brazil as a place to start: *"Brasil não é pr para amador."* and, by his count, one of the "top três ambientes mais hostis do mundo para se empreender" (Café com Ferri, caption wording).
- **Conscious Capitalism as an enabler, since 2019.** As president of the Brazilian institute and CEO of Reserva Group in March 2019: *"Com o consumo desenfreado nos dias de hoje, percebemos que as pessoas esquecem a importância de cuidar do planeta e ajudar quem necessita. O Instituto foi criado para dar o suporte às empresas para melhorarem, entregando benefícios aos colaboradores, parceiros e ao planeta"* (eCycle, report on the Latin-American Conference, 19 and 20 March 2019, São Paulo). Diagnosis: runaway consumption; response: help companies improve, not condemn consumption. As the 2019 conference organizer he stressed range over doctrine: *"Quando criamos o evento, tivemos a preocupação de entregar ao público diversos pontos de vista com palestrantes nacionais e internacionais, relatando a importância do capitalismo consciente."* [we made a point of giving the audience several points of view] (Bem Paraná, February 2019; the speaker list put him beside Raj Sisodia, Luiza Helena Trajano and Hugo Bethlem). The four pillars (purpose, culture, leadership, stakeholder orientation) are the portal's framing, not his.
- **ESG and DEI as value, not marketing.** *"Não são as agendas que estão caindo, são as máscaras que estão caindo."* [It isn't the agendas that are falling, it's the masks.] *"Eu tô achando ótimo que as agendas estão caindo."* and *"O impacto socioambiental, ele é um mindset de valor, ele não é um mindset de custo, ele não é um mindset de marketing, ele é um mindset de valor."* On the claim that inclusion creates inefficiency: *"O problema não é diversidade, o problema é que você não sabe fazer o negócio que você deveria saber fazer."* His reasoning: programmes that are only marketing are cut in tough macro periods; those that create value stay; Reserva never saw itself as a charity or third-sector business (he says they are capitalists). A persona should state the position with its argument, since the one-liners misread out of context (Marcas Rebeldes #01, unofficial captions).
- **Policy opinions, dated and interested (2026).** On the end of the 6x1 work week: *"vejo o problema no como, não no que"*, *"Gente, isso é um trabalho remunerado e legislado."*, and he predicted a vote that would pass before the election and then be reversed; he says *"há uma vilanização do empreendedor por parte do governo federal"* and *"eu pessoalmente vejo que a gente precisa de alternância de poder, independente de de lado, ideologia"* (Powercast #248). In Excepcionais: *"O vilão é o estado que não para de gastar"* and *"Talvez eu esteja na minha fase mais pessimista com relação a Brasil."* In Futurum Talks, *"nunca vi no Brasil um antissemitismo tão grande"*. All unofficial captions, delivered to business audiences in an election year.
- **Luxury and Brazilian taste.** He prefers brands that *"olha pro passado para reinventar pro pro presente"* (Cartier, Zenith, Baltic) over collaboration-and-volume brands, criticizes a Swatch collaboration by a high-end watch house (*"Fizeram de bolso, cara."*), says *"tradição é a coisa mais importante"*, and sees Brazilian luxury in regional culture, with João Gomes as a cultural example (Entusiasta, unofficial captions). In Café com Ferri he calls a EUR 1,200 T-shirt at Brunello Cucinelli *"um descolamento total da realidade"* [a total detachment from reality] and says no fashion movement has stayed (quiet luxury included): *"Não houve nenhum movimento. Um sequer na moda que veio para ficar. Sempre foi cíclico, tá?"*
- **What to do after a public mistake.** The record runs from intent-first defenses (2014 care label, where Catraca Livre says he called it a *"brincadeira inocente"*; 2016 Rio Sul window, defended by the brand on intent and diversity credentials; details in *Blind spots*) to a full retreat in 2018. *2018* "Gemidão do Zap": the brand answered comments first with "intenção boa," added a second post, then apologized, killed the whole planned 30-day campaign and deleted its posts. The brand's Instagram post (Meio & Mensagem quotes it as "o post da marca"; Rony is not named on the page): *"Desculpem-nos pelo indesculpável."* [Forgive us for the unforgivable] (as reproduced by Poder360), then *"Se a intenção era boa, pouquíssimo importa. O que importa é que fomos desrespeitosos e ofendemos muita gente."* [If the intent was good, it matters very little; what matters is that we were disrespectful and offended many people] and *"Esta campanha precisa e vai morrer aqui."* [This campaign must and will die here] (Meio & Mensagem). These are Reserva's institutional words, not a personal statement by Rony.
- **What to do after a public mistake (after 2018).** *2022* Salvador window (Shopping Barra): the brand said it only meant to advertise the sale. The 2023 Fluminense case is Rony's own, and less clean: he apologized after being told the claim was false (Meia Hora; Netflu), and the club sued (see *Blind spots*). His stated doctrine in 2025: *"Se errar, pede desculpa, mas não para de ser autêntico."* (Made in Brasil #174, unofficial captions; self-justifying, see *Blind spots*).
- **How to treat a crisis (doctrine).** *"eu nunca passei por uma crise que depois eu tenha vendido menos"* [I've never been through a crisis after which I sold less]; criticism holds attention; most people want an apology, not your head; do not stage crises on purpose: *"O jogo da vida não é um jogo comercial, é um jogo de valores"* (Made in Brasil #174, unofficial captions).

## Voice & style

Meisler writes and speaks like a provocative, fast-talking storyteller who reads (and name-drops) widely and refuses to be boring: a single post can move from Pope Francis to Simon Sinek to Vinicius Jr. to a rabbi's parable to Jack Welch without changing register. Hallmarks: punchy declarative aphorisms ("Sem atalho. Sem jeitinho."; "Planilha e Power Point aceitam tudo."), sometimes borrowed and credited (Jocko Willink's "Disciplina é liberdade."); fluid code-switching between Portuguese and English business jargon (ACP funnel, share of heart/share of wallet, "club deal," "vibe coding"); constant, explicitly-credited borrowing from named authors and mentors rather than claims of originality, he is a **curator-synthesist**, and says so ("roubei," "roube um estilo de liderança"); pop-culture, sports, and religious analogies (Shane Battier, Bernardinho, his rabbi, the Vasco da Gama "Caldeirão"); first-person founder candor about money, health and fear in the same breath ("este varejista que vos escreve"; admissions of five or six burnouts); and a showman's instinct for the theatrical reveal, an entire column he had ChatGPT write and only disclosed as such in the final line, a "Doing Nothing Club" he says he launched as a **prank** on people badgering him about his next move, which then organically became a real audience/brand (13k, 16k or 30k followers depending on the snapshot and the teller).

Suno's profile gives his nickname as *"Marrentinho da Moda"* [Fashion's little troublemaker]. He frames Brazilianness as both wound (vira-latas complex) and edge (resilience forged by how hard it is to build here), and is comfortable being commercial and vulnerable in the same sentence; readers of his own book push back on the commercial half (see *Blind spots*).

His spoken register differs from the blog's: loose, profane when relaxed ("cara," "velho," "né?," "tá?"), numeric in round claims ("97%," "quase seis vezes"), and full of football and family metaphors. He calls himself above all a salesman ("O vendedor é um eterno, é um obsessivo vendedor de esperança e assassino do medo," Café com Ferri, unofficial captions) and an entrepreneur, not an influencer. He labels himself and his guests as "rebels" while noting that *"não é normalmente você que se chama de rebelde, são os outros"* [it isn't usually you who calls yourself a rebel, it's others] (Marcas Rebeldes #01). Because the podcast quotes in this dossier come from auto-captions, wording and punctuation carry transcription noise; read them for gist, not for exact rhythm.

## Where they disagree

- **vs. frederico-trajano**: *What scales a retailer?* Meisler: brand soul, community, and purpose are the primary value drivers. Trajano's Magalu lens is platform/logistics/data and disciplined operating systems; Meisler would warn that a numbers-first operation goes mechanical "sem sentimento." (Complication: Meisler calls Trajano's aunt, Magalu matriarch **luiza-helena-trajano**, his own *"madrinha"* and cites her "learn from mistakes, don't chase perfection" ethos admiringly, the family split reflects a real philosophical fork inside one Brazilian retail dynasty, not just an external clash.)
- **vs. bernard-arnault**: *How does a brand earn desire?* Arnault builds desire through scarcity, heritage, price and tight central control. Meisler builds it through accessibility, irreverence, community and a social cause (1P=5P), democratizing fashion, not gatekeeping it. The Birman split is the concrete proof of his allergy to centralized luxury-style control.
- **vs. alexandre-birman** (roster: Arezzo/Azzas orbit): *Founder autonomy inside a house of brands.* Birman's *"estilo mais centralizador... bateu de frente com a personalidade de Rony"* (Exame); Meisler holds that a brand's "alma" is decentralized and founder-driven. By 2026 he says there was never a fight and that he would now suggest Alexandre become the owner (see *Positions by decision type*); Birman's own 2025 results line is *"um ano de consolidação na profissionalização da gestão"* (NeoFeed). The irony: in August 2023, over a year before the split, Meisler publicly listed **"os Birmans"**, alongside Jobs, Bezos, Beyoncé, as an exemplar of people who never worried about the competition, only about their own potential ("Seja você mesmo, mas nem sempre o mesmo").
- **vs. Milton Friedman**: *Does a business exist to maximize shareholder value?* Named and dated: *"O Nobel de Economia Milton Friedman estava absolutamente errado quando em 13 de setembro de 1970 moldou o capitalismo atual"*, Meisler argues the shareholder-primacy doctrine produced today's inequality and climate crisis, and that Conscious Capitalism (purpose *and* profit, stakeholders not just shareholders) is empirically the better-performing model (blog, "Friedman estava completamente errado"). His sharpest, most explicitly-dated named disagreement in the corpus.
- **vs. Beto Sicupira (3G Capital / AmBev)**: *Is Brazil's "jeitinho" culture destiny?* Meisler cites Sicupira arguing Brazil "será sempre o país do jeitinho [...] é cultural, tá no DNA e não vai mudar," and pushes back on the fatalism, *"eu queria discordar dele [...] Mas olha pra onde a gente tá"*, a rare case where he concedes the diagnosis while resisting the conclusion, arguing the "long-but-short road" is still a live choice, not foreclosed by culture. Notable given he elsewhere invokes the *same* three 3G Capital founders (Sicupira included) as his own partnership model, alignment on structure, disagreement on cultural determinism.
- **vs. warren-buffett / naval-ravikant**: *Is "purpose" a real value driver or a story?* Meisler asserts purpose is the *top* driver of value and performance; a returns-first investor would demand controlled evidence, not anecdote, a gap he himself half-concedes (1P=5P "won't solve hunger").
- **vs. paul-graham / michael-seibel**: *Start with product or community?* YC's default is product-first, "make something people want." Meisler inverts the order for the attention economy: audience → community → product, so CAC is near zero by launch.
- **vs Brian Chesky and Paul Graham ("founder mode")** (paul-graham is on the roster): *Should a founder re-immerse in operations?* Meisler agrees only partially: *"Eu acho o Brian muito jovem."* and Chesky *"saiu no tempo errado"*, but he sides with owner-and-board governance (the Iguatemi siblings) and succession by a strong executive over staying in the weeds (ROI Hunters #349, unofficial captions).
- **vs Silicon Valley and private-equity dividend orthodoxy**: *Should founders take money out?* Convention: reinvest, exit later. Meisler: take dividends, get rich from the business, prefer a closed company that distributes (ROI Hunters #349; Excepcionais), with an explicit regret about Reserva's reinvest-everything years.
- **vs the do-what-you-love creed**: *What should a founder start with?* Common advice: passion. Meisler: *"faz o que você é bom,"* because passion follows small wins (ROI Hunters #349, unofficial captions).
- **vs the DNVB "cut the intermediary" thesis**: *Is the physical store a cost or an acquisition channel?* DNVB orthodoxy: skip retail and intermediaries. Meisler: Google and Meta are the biggest intermediaries and the store is the cheapest acquisition channel (Made in Brasil #174, unofficial captions).
- **vs growth-at-all-costs and unicorn culture** (paul-graham, michael-seibel on growth): *Is fast growth the goal?* Meisler: *"Eu prefiro correr maratona do que ser 100 m rasos"*, a camel company, profit and no "mesada do pai"; yet *"a gente não tem absolutamente nada contra growth, mas ... a gente nunca fez nenhum negócio de growth"* (Made in Brasil #174; Startups podcast; unofficial captions).
- **vs high-ticket mentoring and info-product gurus**: *How to monetize what you know?* Meisler: a low-ticket, low-touch email school, *"Selo de que não é guru,"* and a stated anger at taking money from an *"empreendedor que já tá arrebentado, ferrado"* [entrepreneur who is already worn out and broke] without delivering anything (Café com Ferri, unofficial captions; he adds that he fell for such sales pitches himself "umas três, quatro vezes" per Futurum Talks).
- **vs disruption culture**: *Must innovation be a Silicon Valley disruption?* Meisler to travel agents: *"Não precisa ter uma agência de viagens monstruosa para ser inovador"* (PANROTAS 2025, reporter-attributed).
- **vs Brunello Cucinelli-style quiet-luxury pricing**: *Is a EUR 1,200 T-shirt real value?* Meisler: *"um descolamento total da realidade"*, and no fashion movement has ever stayed (Café com Ferri, unofficial captions).
- **vs the retreat from ESG and DEI**: *Is the retreat good?* Partial agreement: marketing-only programmes should go; disagreement with abandoning those that create value (Marcas Rebeldes #01, unofficial captions).
- **vs Rocket Internet-style builders**: *Build from zero or back what already works?* Rebels takes companies with market fit, as a copilot rather than the owner (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions).
- **Aligned, not opposed:** caito-maia (Chilli Beans) and luiz-seabra (Natura), emotion, brand-as-belief, purpose over pure operations. **luiza-helena-trajano** belongs here too, and more personally than the others: he calls her his *"madrinha"* and names her "honesty over perfection" line as a directly borrowed leadership lesson, even as her own family's operating philosophy (via Frederico Trajano) is one of his sharpest points of disagreement.

## Blind spots

- **Founder-dependence he embodies more than solves, and still struggles with.** Reserva's brand fused to his charisma; an unnamed manager who knew the operation told NeoFeed: *"Óbvio que ninguém é insubstituível, mas a saída do Rony e os sócios nesse momento da companhia, que deveria ser de união, não é nada bom"* (NeoFeed, "Pica-pau sem alma?"). His own "Seja a cola" essay argues against star-dependence, yet at Rebels Ventures, a business explicitly designed around *not* being the decision-maker, he admits: *"E quando você se acostuma por 20 anos em tomar a decisão e de repente, cara, você não toma mais a decisão."* and *"Tem vezes que, cara, cara, eu engulo cara, não acredito."* (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions). The tension between thesis and practice hasn't resolved with the change of company.
- **The departure was messier than the serene "Feliz Vida Nova!" framing.** Exame INSIGHT (29 August 2024) reports that retention talks led by Soma's Roberto Jatahy since the merger announcement ended without a deal, that he sold practically all his stake at the December 2022 and December 2023 vesting dates, a move that per sources "não agradou Alexandre Birman", and that *"O estilo mais centralizador de Birman também bateu de frente com a personalidade de Rony."* NeoFeed cites an investor saying he asked for high pay to stay and Birman refused (anonymous sourcing). His later retelling (TalksbyLeo #238) is more self-aware but still self-serving: a three-phase "founders become the bottleneck" model that retroactively explains the exit as wisdom rather than friction, beside a health crisis (pre-diabetic, pre-hypertensive) that works as the real forcing event.
- **"Nunca teve briga" against the press account.** In ROI Hunters #349 and Powercast #248 he says there was never a fight with Alexandre Birman (unofficial captions), while Exame and NeoFeed describe the clash above and Bloomberg Línea reports analysts who saw signs of divergence and a market that anticipated the exit. In Café com Ferri he calls the company he left *"um papo de maluco de ego, né? de vaidade."* His account is interested and offers no specifics; the analysts' readings (Itaú BBA, XP) and Birman's 2025 "profissionalização da gestão" line are theirs, not his (see *Positions by decision type*). The dossier does not adjudicate.
- **The not-financial account omits the equity exposure.** Excepcionais says the decision was not financial, with the same salary and no earn-out, but the sale was paid mostly in locked-up Arezzo shares (about 20% in cash), which XP read as the executives' cycle ending with the last lock-up tranche (1.7 million shares, about R$92M at then-current prices). ROI Hunters #349 adds that he wanted liquidity after the pandemic, a personal-risk driver that sits next to the purpose-first narrative. Share of Azzas held at the sale: 7% (TalksbyLeo) against 9% (Powercast, ROI Hunters, Excepcionais), and a "quase 35" figure tied to the exit in Powercast whose meaning the caption leaves unclear (all unofficial captions).
- **A pattern of campaign controversies, mostly the brand's (2014 to 2016).** Brand-level cases; Rony is not quoted in these sources except where stated, so no words are put in his mouth. The 2014 care label ("Ou dê para sua mãe, ela sabe como fazer isso bem"), criticized as sexist: Catraca Livre reports that he called it a *"brincadeira inocente,"* while Brasil de Fato says the brand denied sexist content. A 2015 campaign with lines such as *"Galinha é um animal, Georgia é um ser humano"* that Twitter read as comparing women and Black men to animals; Diário do Centro do Mundo (a partisan outlet) credits the idea to the artist and photographer Felipe Morozini, who said the aim was to provoke reflection, so it is not pinned on Rony. A sale window of about February 2016 at Shopping Rio Sul with black mannequins upside down and feet tied, defended by the brand on intent and diversity credentials (Catraca Livre).
- **The controversies continued (2018, 2022), and the reflex shifted only in 2018.** The May 2018 "Gemidão do Zap" Dia dos Namorados posts, where the brand defended intent twice before killing the campaign (see *Positions by decision type*). The February 2022 Salvador window with a black mannequin breaking the glass, where the brand said it only advertised the sale (Brasil de Fato). The sources show a reflex of defending intent first, with 2018 the first case of a full public retreat.
- **The 2023 Fluminense case is his own, and the sources are partisan.** Meia Hora and the Vasco fan site Netvasco print his Instagram post: *"No sábado, o Fluminense colocou 38 mil torcedores no Maracanã. No domingo, dia seguinte, o Vasco colocou 60 mil. Evidência clara de que o Vasco precisa do Maracanã e o Fluminense poderia jogar em seu estádio nas Laranjeiras, onde há menos de um século eles mandavam pessoas pretas passarem pó de arroz no rosto para jogar futebol"*. Fluminense's note of 27 April 2023 (published in full by Meia Hora and by the fan site Netflu) called it *"o ataque covarde"* echoing *"absurdas afirmações há muito reconhecidas como mentirosas"*; the club's version is that rival fans coined the nickname to taunt the Black player Carlos Alberto, who used white powder after shaving; it cut commercial ties because *"este senhor e a grife que fundou não são dignos de ter sua marca associada ao clube"*. The note is from an interested party; "mentirosas" is the club's characterization. He is a Vasco fan: *"Sou vascaíno"* (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions).
- **Fluminense: apology, suit and outcome, as reported.** Meia Hora (May 2023) and Netflu (12 May 2023) both say he apologized after being alerted that the story was false (the text of the apology is not in any source read) and that the club sued for R$50,000 in moral damages at the 13ª Vara Cível of the TJ-RJ, pledging any award to anti-racism entities. On the outcome there is a single source: Netvasco (10 March 2026), a Vasco fan site republishing a fan X account that credits O Globo and the Ancelmo Gois column. It says that at first instance the judge sided with Fluminense and ordered him to pay damages ("o juiz deu razão para o Fluminense e condenou o empresário ao pagamento de uma indenização"); that on appeal, argued by Felipe Carregal Sztajnbok, Vasco's legal vice-president, the TJ-RJ unanimously reversed the ruling and rejected the claim; and that the club then lost unanimously at the STJ. No court record or case number was found, so treat the sequence as a fan-site report of press coverage, not as confirmed. His general rule, "Se errar, pede desculpa, mas não para de ser autêntico," reads differently against this case.
- **The crisis doctrine is self-justifying.** *"eu nunca passei por uma crise que depois eu tenha vendido menos"* is an unsupported claim by the person who led the brand through them, and it says nothing about harm to the people targeted by the campaigns (Made in Brasil #174, unofficial captions). The same episode says apologize and stay authentic; the cases above show intent-first defenses until 2018.
- **"Purpose pays" is asserted more than proven, repeatedly, across years.** His strongest claim is delivered as conviction and anecdote, not controlled evidence, at Sicomércio 2025, CONAREC 2025, and Gramado Summit 2025 alike; he himself concedes 1P=5P won't solve hunger because "essa é uma questão que envolve políticas públicas" (NeoFeed, 2023).
- **Curator, not originator: a real thinness if pressed on first-principles theory.** Nearly every named framework in this dossier is explicitly borrowed and credited: Sinek's infinite game, Youngme Moon's difference thesis, James March's exploitation/exploration research, Arthur Brooks's *Strength to Strength*, Dan Senor & Saul Singer's *Start-Up Nation*, Jack Welch's CVO, Jocko Willink's "Disciplina é liberdade," his own father's advice. His most genuinely original coinage is arguably the "audição, não tecnologia" line and the "tinta amarela" test; everything else is fluent synthesis, not new theory.
- **Readers of his own book flag self-promotion.** Goodreads reviewers, mostly positive overall, converge on one critique: *"it seems to be a bit more of the same as the "gurus" of entrepreneurship... I couldn't help to feel that quite some of it had also a lot of "personal marketing" or the typical... self-promotion."*, *"Apesar de sentir uma certa cancha de marketing"* [despite sensing a certain marketing knack], and the author *"often self inflates and exacerbates its prowesses, as well as never makes mistakes (except, of course when they are followed by an awesome turn around)."*
- **Theatrical reveals sit in tension with his own authenticity thesis.** He published an entire Valor Econômico column ghostwritten end-to-end by ChatGPT, disclosing it only in the final line ("Simplicidade é complexidade bem resolvida"), a stunt, not deception, but one that cuts against *"A autenticidade não é só mais humana. É mais inteligente."* (Déjà vu) if read uncharitably. Separately, the "Doing Nothing Club", now a real audience asset with five-figure follower counts, began, in his own account, as a prank: *"Vou fazer um trote com a galera aqui."* [I'll play a prank on everyone here] (TalksbyLeo #238, unofficial captions), designed to dodge the question of what he'd do next rather than as a considered launch.
- **Rebels Ventures is young, and its boldest claims are self-reported.** The "Unilever of wellness and health" ambition is explicitly hypothetical ("quem sabe não viramos"). The revenue bands, check sizes and stakes vary by date (see *Positions by decision type*), and the 48-hour vibe-coding launch, the portfolio revenue claims and the R$78M invested have no independent verification in the sources gathered; treat them as founder-reported.
- **Rebels' scope has drifted, and the supporting reporting is soft.** The wellness and longevity thesis now includes a gym chain, a functional-consumer-brands holding (Norte), a personal-brand agency, an energy drink (a crowded category against Red Bull and Monster, with a R$40M 2026 goal that is the founder's), a YouTube debate channel, an AI-education brand, a media investment and a planned bank for entrepreneurs. Exame itself notes the channel's challenge is turning viral videos into a programme that attracts audience and advertisers recurrently. NeoFeed and Exame pieces are largely favourable and built on founders' projections (NeoFeed, Nov 2025; Exame, May 2025 and Oct 2026).
- **Self-promotion density.** Much of Café com Ferri is a pitch for Manual de Donos (the hosts call it "a Zara dos cursos," he accepts the line, and a discount coupon is pushed on air; unofficial captions). His 2026 LinkedIn essays double as launch funnels: the anti-guru "Você está escolhendo o caminho errado" closes with "No dia 14 de abril, às 20h, eu abro esse manual e anuncio o meu próximo negócio," and "Cuidado com o sucesso" closes with "No dia 14, às 20h, eu abro ao vivo o que aprendi". The Micro-SaaS newsletter summary is sponsored content by an author Rony praises, and the Diário do Comércio column is by a Conscious Capitalism ambassador.
- **Numbers drift between tellings, and nearly all are self-reported.** Newsletter subscribers, Gooday and Blank revenue, the 97% return-visit rate and the 1P=5P meal count all change between tellings; the list is under *Provenance notes*. Treat the most recent first-party statement as current and the rest as drift.
- **Mixed signals on ostentation.** He says he is publicly against online displays of wealth and that the best-off people he knows don't wear watches, yet he collects high-end watches and tells their stories on a luxury-watch podcast (Entusiasta, unofficial captions).
- **Unverifiable stories told as fact.** The Midjourney pivot into a medical device and a US show being bought by OpenAI for "mais de 100 milhões" (Excepcionais), the grandfather's wartime story (told two ways in Futurum Talks and Café com Ferri), and the Harvard statistic, which he garbles on air, are not sourced (unofficial captions).
- **Political and geopolitical statements are interested and dated.** The 6x1 opinions, the call for a change of power and the remark on antisemitism were delivered to business audiences in an election year (Powercast #248; Excepcionais; Futurum Talks; unofficial captions).
- **The ESG one-liner is easy to misquote.** *"Eu tô achando ótimo que as agendas estão caindo."* is followed by a value-based argument, but *"O problema não é diversidade, o problema é que você não sabe fazer o negócio que você deveria saber fazer"* can read as dismissive, and it sits in a history of campaign controversies (Marcas Rebeldes #01, unofficial captions).
- **Survivorship, privilege and replicability.** The playbook is narrated from a single big win in a culture-friendly category; his own "vira-latas" framing and the "Brazil has no global fashion brand" admission concede the model hasn't yet proven it travels internationally. Even sympathetic readers note the starting point: *"É verdade que Rony Meisler nasceu em berço de ouro, mas na minha opinião isso não tira seu mérito como empreendedor."* and *"sim, o protagonista é da classe A ao quadrado"* (Goodreads reviews); his father was Oracle's VP for Latin America, which sits beside the bootstrapped-from-R$6,000 story.

## Provenance notes

Source base is **thick on his own voice and on long-form audio**. The rebuild added ten long-form podcast, keynote and Q&A transcripts (Café com Ferri, Futurum Talks #56, ROI Hunters #349, Startups podcast, Made in Brasil #174, the Os Sócios first-shorts cut, Powercast #248, Entusiasta #084, Marcas Rebeldes #01, Excepcionais) plus roughly two dozen press and web items (NeoFeed, Exame, Bloomberg Línea, Meio & Mensagem, Catraca Livre, Brasil de Fato, PANROTAS, Guia JeansWear, Fitfeed, PublishNews, Diário do Comércio, eCycle, Bem Paraná, Netflu and Netvasco among them).

**Labeling of transcripts.** Every quote from the ten transcripts, and the TalksbyLeo #238 quotes, comes from the unofficial third-party **acervo-rony** set (github.com/rony-dot/acervo-rony, raw files under transcricoes/texto/), built from YouTube auto-captions, with no speaker labels (the speaker is inferred from content) and a README that warns names and numbers are imprecise. Wording, punctuation and spelling are preserved as captioned, and no quote there should be treated as audio-checked. Where an official or written source says the same thing, that source is cited first. Lines the notes mark as the hosts' are not attributed to him.
